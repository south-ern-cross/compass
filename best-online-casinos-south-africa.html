Why This Page Has No Ranking

Our normal ranking weighs licensing, local payments, withdrawal speed and bonus fairness - but only for jurisdictions where players can legally use the casinos. South Africa fails that threshold: the National Gambling Act 7 of 2004 (Section 11) prohibits engaging in interactive games, the 2008 Amendment Act that would have licensed online gambling was never brought into force, and the National Gambling Board reaffirmed in February 2026 that online casino gambling is illegal with enforcement stepping up. Participating as a player is itself an offence, and winnings from unlawful gambling can be forfeited to the state under Section 16.
