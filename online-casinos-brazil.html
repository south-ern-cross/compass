How It Works

Law 14.790/2023 - the "Lei das Bets" - brought fixed-odds sports betting and online casino games (slots, roulette, blackjack, crash games) under federal regulation. The market went live on 1 January 2025 under the Secretaria de Prêmios e Apostas (SPA), part of the Ministry of Finance. Operators need a Brazilian legal entity, local servers, strict KYC and responsible-gaming systems, and must pay a R$30 million license fee plus 12% tax on gross gaming revenue. Players face 15% income tax on winnings above R$2,112.

2026 has been the enforcement year. As of June 2026, 78 licensed operators running 138 brands were authorized. The SPA activated domain blocking with ANATEL (Brazil's telecom regulator) in January 2026, and by March 2026 more than 2,100 unlicensed gambling domains had been blocked inside Brazil. Payment processors - including PIX - were ordered to refuse transactions to unlicensed operators (consolidated in Ordinance SPA/MF No. 2,750/2026, which requires financial institutions to monitor and report signs of unauthorized gambling activity). In September 2026 the SPA ordered the immediate suspension of 14 licensed sites over failures in the Sigap real-time monitoring system and responsible-gaming controls.

The framework is also politically unstable. A bill (PL-1808/2026, backed by 68 lawmakers) was filed to repeal the Bets Law entirely, and in September 2026 Reuters reported the government was drafting a provisional measure that could ban online casino operations and restrict sports betting - a move that would upend the licensed market if enacted. Fines of up to R$2 billion were floated in the draft. The situation can change within weeks, so treat any statement about Brazilian legality as time-stamped.

For individual players: playing at an SPA-licensed .bet.br operator with PIX is the only clearly compliant route. Offshore casinos licensed in Curaçao or elsewhere hold no Brazilian license; they may be domain-blocked or have their PIX rails cut at any time. Several major offshore casinos also geo-block Brazil themselves - BitStarz, Cashy and N1 Casino all list Brazil on their official restricted-countries lists.

Payment Methods

PIX is the dominant payment rail in Brazil - the Central Bank's instant, 24/7 transfer system, used for the vast majority of legal gambling deposits and withdrawals. Licensed operators may only accept PIX, TED and debit/prepaid cards: Normative Ordinance No. 615/2024 explicitly bans crypto, credit cards, BNPL, cash, payment slips and cheques for licensed operators, and requires player accounts to be in the customer's own name at a Central Bank-authorized institution.

FAQ

Is online casino gambling legal in Brazil?

Yes, but only at SPA-licensed operators under Law 14.790/2023. Offshore unlicensed casinos face ANATEL domain blocking and PIX payment blacklisting. The framework is politically contested - a ban bill and a reported provisional measure in 2026 could change the picture.

Can I use crypto or a credit card to deposit?

Not at licensed operators: Normative Ordinance No. 615/2024 bans crypto, credit cards, BNPL, cash and payment slips. Only PIX, TED and debit/prepaid cards are allowed.

What is PIX?

PIX is Brazil's instant payment system, run by the Central Bank - free, 24/7 transfers between bank accounts, and the standard rail for legal gambling deposits and withdrawals.
