Overview

Propaganda 2 takes ELK Studios back to the dystopian city of LA11-1, where the propaganda of love just got louder. ELK Studios lists the release for 22 October 2026. The game runs on a 6x6 grid with cluster pays, and the whole design is built around uprising: multiplying wilds, synced symbols and a set of bonus rounds that feed off each other.

The headline mechanic is the Love Bomb. Energy attached to feature symbols adds to the Love Bomb meter, and when it triggers, every feature symbol stays active for the rest of the round (see FAQ). The Wild Strike drops wilds onto the grid, the Heart Feature transforms payout symbols into a single symbol, and collecting 6 masks opens The Drop, where you upgrade and collect a payout symbol while multiplying wilds grow the clusters.

Three Bonus symbols open the Bonus Game with a persistent global multiplier; if one of them is a Super Bonus symbol, the Super Bonus starts with the Love Bomb active throughout. The X-iter menu offers 5 buy-in modes from 2.5x to 500x the bet where permitted. At a 25,000x top prize, this is a chase for the biggest multipliers rather than a casual spin.

Propaganda 2 FAQ

What is the RTP of Propaganda 2?

Published pre-release game data lists a 96% headline RTP for Propaganda 2, but ELK Studios ships the game in several configurations and each casino picks one. The exact figure can differ between casinos, so check the game info or paytable inside the game where you play.

How does the Love Bomb work in Propaganda 2?

Energy attached to some feature symbols adds to the Love Bomb energy. When the Love Bomb triggers, it activates all feature symbols for the remainder of the game round. In the Super Bonus, the Love Bomb stays active for the entire bonus.
