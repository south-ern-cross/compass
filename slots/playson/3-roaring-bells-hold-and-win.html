Overview

Flaming bells ring against a classic fruit-machine backdrop, with the fire cranked all the way up.

3 Roaring Bells: Hold and Win is a Playson slot, released on 15 October 2026. It keeps the old-school 3x3 grid with 5 paylines and wraps it in a fiery bells-and-fruit theme.

Playson lists the RTP at 95.51% with high volatility. The official press release confirms four jackpots - Mini, Minor, Major and Grand - worth up to 1,000x the bet.

Multi-coloured Bell symbols land in the main game and get collected by Bells above the reels. Special Bells bring the heat: the red Expand Bell adds 2 rows, the green Double Bell marks 4 cells (6 with Expand active) and doubles symbol values, and the blue Extra Bell opens a new round while keeping active features. The Bonus Game starts with 3 respins that reset on every new Bell, and Collect symbols on the middle reel gather Bell values up to 8x. A standard Bonus Game buy option is available, plus a Super Bonus Game at 200x the bet with all three features active.
