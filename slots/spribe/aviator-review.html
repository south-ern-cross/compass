How It Works

Aviator is a crash game, not a slot - there are no reels or paylines. Each round, a plane takes off and a multiplier climbs from 1.00x upward. You place your bet before the round starts, then cash out at any moment while the plane is still flying. Cash out in time and you win your stake multiplied by the current multiplier; wait too long and the plane flies away, taking the whole stake with it.

Rounds are short - typically 8 to 15 seconds - and you can place two bets per round, letting you bank one early while letting the second ride for a bigger multiplier. Auto cash-out and auto play are built in, so you can lock a target multiplier in advance instead of reacting in real time.

About 3% of rounds crash instantly at 1.00x, before anyone can act - that instant-bust rate is how the 3% house edge is distributed. The multiplier itself is theoretically uncapped, but the maximum payout is limited to $10,000 per bet.

Provably Fair

Aviator uses provably fair cryptography: Spribe publishes a hash of the server seed before each round opens, and the seed is revealed after the round ends, so every result can be independently verified. No outcome can be predicted in advance - beware of "predictor" apps and signal groups, which cannot see the seed before the round closes.

The game also has social features: live bets from other players are visible during each round, there is an in-game chat, and operators can drop free bets through the "Rain" feature.
