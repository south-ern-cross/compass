Overview

Nunchucks from NetEnt turns a quiet parish into the scene of a cartoon defence. Sinpin and his gang have their eyes on the church communion boxes, and the sisters guarding them are not the quiet types the gang expected. Mentored by Mother Sofia, Sisters Faith, Hope and Love dust off their old training and fight back, a rosary in one hand and a pinless grenade in the other, in NetEnt's own telling of the game.

The joke of the game is the contrast. Everything about the setting says patience and prayer, and everything about the sisters says otherwise. The official page puts it plainly: the time for turning the other cheek is over. That clash between habit and combat training carries the whole theme, and it is what makes Nunchucks stand out from the usual heist and adventure slots around it.

What the official page does not give is numbers. NetEnt's Nunchucks page publishes no grid size, no RTP, no volatility rating, no maximum win, no stake range and no release date, so none appear in the table above.
