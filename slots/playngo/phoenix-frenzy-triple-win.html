Overview

Play'n GO sends three phoenixes rising from a volcanic landscape of molten lava and jagged mountains. The studio's official game page lists Phoenix Frenzy Triple Win for release on 19 November 2026. Each phoenix carries its own power: the Purple Phoenix transforms symbols into wilds, the Blue Phoenix commands multiplying wilds, and the Golden Phoenix upgrades royal symbols into golden versions of itself.

Above the reels sit three Blazing Egg Meters, one for each phoenix. Coloured Mystery Orbs landing on the reels feed the matching meter, and collecting them can wake one, two or all three Rebirth features. Every Rebirth awards 8 free spins: Purple Rebirth turns 1 to 5 royals on reels 2 to 5 into wilds, Blue Rebirth upgrades wilds into x2 Double Wilds, and Golden Rebirth upgrades a royal into Golden Phoenix symbols, with 3 more orbs retriggering 3 extra spins and waking another royal, up to 30 free spins.

The Triple Win twist is that rebirths can combine: Purple plus Blue mixes wild transformations with multipliers, Purple plus Golden mixes wilds with upgrades, and all three together stack every power at once (see FAQ). Play'n GO's page does not publish an RTP or max win for this title yet, so check the in-game info once it goes live.
