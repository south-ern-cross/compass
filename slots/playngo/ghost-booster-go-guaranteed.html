Overview

A friendly blue-white ghost named Wispy drifts beside the reels, gathering every coin within reach of a haunted mansion.

Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed is a Play'n GO slot released on 1 October 2026. It plays on 5 reels and 4 rows with 20 fixed paylines, set in a ghostly mansion under a neon night sky.

Play'n GO lists the RTP at up to 96.25% (lower settings exist at some casinos), volatility is medium, and the maximum win is 5,000x the bet.

Coin and Collector symbols drive the game: Collectors gather Coin values in the base game and in Hold & Spin. Six or more Coins trigger the Hold & Spin bonus with 3 respins, and the GO Guaranteed meter sets a minimum payout for the round - if the result falls below the meter's value, the game lifts it to the guaranteed amount. Wispy's coin collection can also activate the Wheel of Spooks jackpot feature with four ghost-themed jackpot tiers.

Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed FAQ

What is the RTP of Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed?

Play'n GO lists the RTP of Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed at up to 96.25%. Some casinos may use a lower setting, so check the in-game info at the casino where you play.

How does the GO Guaranteed meter work?

The meter builds up during the base game as Collector symbols land without Coins. When the Hold & Spin bonus ends, if the total is below the meter's value, the game raises the payout to the guaranteed minimum.

What is the maximum win in Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed?

The maximum win is 5,000x the bet.

Does Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Play'n GO releases, Ghost Booster! GO Guaranteed runs in the browser on phones and tablets, with no download needed.
