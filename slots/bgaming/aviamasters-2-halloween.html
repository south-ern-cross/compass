Overview

A pumpkinhead pilot takes off into haunted skies while ghosts drift across the flight path, ready to cut the journey short.

Aviamasters 2 Halloween is the Halloween edition of BGaming's casual hit Aviamasters 2, released on 15 October 2026. It is a crash-style game rather than a traditional slot: you set a stake, the plane takes off on a random flight path, and the round pays only if the plane lands on an island rather than in the sea.
