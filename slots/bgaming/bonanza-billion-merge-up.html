Overview

Bonanza Billion Merge Up+ from BGaming, released on 24 September 2026, takes the familiar fruit-and-gem look of the Bonanza Billion series and rebuilds its engine around the studio's Merge Up mechanic. BGaming says the rework came from real player feedback: instead of a plain cascade, every win now feeds back into the grid, so a single spin can keep paying while the board reshuffles itself.

Wins pay anywhere: 8 or more identical symbols anywhere on the reels pay, with no paylines to follow. Winning symbols then merge into a new symbol, one of the types already on the reels, and the more of a type that is present, the greater the chance the merge lands on that type. Empty spaces refill with symbols falling from above, and refilling keeps going until no new winning combination forms.

Scatters open the free spins round: 4 Scatters give 10 free spins, 5 give 20 and 6 give 30. Multiplier symbols from 2x to 100x can appear, and when several appear they are added together and applied to the total win of the spin. Landing 3 or more Scatters during the round adds 5 more free spins, and multipliers can also appear at random after tumbling sequences or merges. Players who want to skip ahead can use Buy Bonus for free spins or Chance x2 for a better shot at triggering them, where the local licence allows it.

The headline numbers are a 97.04% RTP, very high volatility and a maximum win of 15,000x the stake, with BGaming also listing a 255,000 euro maximum win figure. BGaming's game page points players to its own demo, and the game runs in the browser on phones and tablets with no download needed. All facts on this page come from BGaming's official game page and its official game rules; the official page publishes no bet range, so check the in-game info for the stakes that apply where you play.
