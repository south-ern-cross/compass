Overview

Black Friday Madness from BGaming is scheduled for release on 18 November 2026, and it takes the year's biggest shopping day as its whole setting. All facts on this page come from BGaming's official game page on bgaming.com. The studio calls it another controversial release in the footsteps of its Ultras slot: an exploration of the chaos inside a consumerist society, a chaos that sharpens around Black Friday, and a game BGaming itself pitches as a social-horror option for Halloween as well.

The shelf is a 5x4 grid holding discounted products and a cast of very colourful characters, each with their own agenda. BGaming asks whether these zealous shoppers will fight for their own gain or join forces to make sure every bit of loot is taken, and the reels sit right in that argument: fierce competition on the surface, shared plunder underneath.

The bonus to watch is the Coin-filled Hold and Win. Land 6 or more gleaming gold Coins and the bonus game opens, with milestones at 10, 15 and 20 Coins and four prizes on the ladder: Mini at 10x, Minor at 25x, Major at 100x and Grand at 500x. On the main reels, Expanding Wild carts add the other headline mechanic. The numbers around it are generous for a medium-volatility game: 60 lines, a 97.00% RTP and a maximum win of 5,000x the stake.

One honest note: BGaming's game page currently marks the demo and trailer as coming soon, so there is no playable preview to check yet. When the studio posts them, this page will be updated from that source.
