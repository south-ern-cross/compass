Overview

Throne of Elements: Platinum drops the Hold the Jackpot formula into deep space. The grid starts partially locked and grows as you play: Guardian symbols landing across the middle row stay sticky and unlock extra reels, opening the way to a full 25-reel layout.

The bonus triggers through the central reel or by completing the Guardian collection. Once it starts, the whole 25-reel grid opens with sticky symbols and respins that reset on every new symbol. The Grand Jackpot reaches 2,500x the bet, with a Grand Mystery symbol offering a second route to the top prize.

Support features keep the base game busy. Cash Infinity symbols lock in place with sticky values, a Cash Out countdown converts Mystery Cash symbols into instant payouts, and Chance Level raises the bonus odds for up to 10x the stake. Extra Life symbols can add up to three lifelines when the bonus respins run dry.
