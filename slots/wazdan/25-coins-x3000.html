Overview

25 Coins x3000 opened Wazdan's Cointober campaign on 1 October 2026, and it is the purest expression of the studio's Coins formula so far. There are no paylines here at all: a 5x5 grid of 25 independent positions exists for one purpose, the Hold the Jackpot bonus round, where every new symbol resets the respins and the grid fills toward the 3,000x Grand Jackpot.

The base game is stripped back to pure anticipation. Coins, Cash symbols and Mystery symbols land and stick, building a board that carries straight into the bonus. Cash Infinity symbols arrive with values of 5x to 10x and stay locked, while Sticky to Infinity keeps Mystery and Jackpot Mystery symbols on the grid from the base game all the way to the final reveal.

It is a high-volatility game by design: long quiet stretches while the board builds, then everything pays out in one explosive bonus round. The enhanced Cluster Collector adds a second layer, gathering values from adjacent Cash and Cash Infinity symbols across up to five spins.

How the mechanics work

Hold the Jackpot (bonus round). The round starts with 3 respins. Every new symbol that lands resets the counter to 3 and locks in place. The round ends when the respins run out or all 25 positions are filled. Fill the whole grid and the 3,000x Grand Jackpot is yours. Mini, Minor and Major Jackpots can also land during the round.

Sticky to Infinity. Mystery and Jackpot Mystery symbols that land in the base game stay locked on the grid through the end of the bonus round. They build the board before the feature even starts and lift the chance of triggering it.

Cash Infinity. Cash Infinity symbols land sticky in the base game carrying values between 5x and 10x the bet. They sit on the grid and their values are paid out during the final reveal of the Hold the Jackpot round.

Cluster Collector. The enhanced Cluster Collector gathers the values of adjacent Cash and Cash Infinity symbols across up to five spins, then pays the combined total when the bonus game ends. It rewards keeping valuable symbols next to each other on the grid.
