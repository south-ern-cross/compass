Overview

Nuclear Tumble is Relax Gaming's fallout-farm slot, made in collaboration with CasinoGrounds, and Relax announced its launch for 29 October 2026. All facts on this page come from Relax Gaming's official game page and the studio's own launch announcement. The setting is a chaotic farm after the fallout, where a pumped-up grizzly grows oversized produce with nuclear waste, and that image sets the whole tone: cheerful farming gone radioactive.

The game page describes fast, simple play: wins cascade, coins pile up, and payouts land quickly and clearly. Payouts use a Ways mechanic, with no fixed payline count stated on the official page, and bets run from 0.20 to 200 euros. That is the full spec the official page gives for now, and this page does not fill the gaps with figures from review sites.

The headline feature in Relax's announcement is Meltdown Mode, new to the Tumble series. Clear every block and the mode triggers: the grid is wiped clean while cash prizes stay in play, and a fresh grid opens the chance of more cash prizes at bigger values. It turns a cleared board into a second chance rather than a dead end.

One honest note: the official page gives no grid size, RTP, volatility or max win, so this page does not guess them. When Relax posts those figures, this page will be updated from that source.
