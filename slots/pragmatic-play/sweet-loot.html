Overview

Sweet Loot from Pragmatic Play, released on 29 October 2026, sends a crew of gummy pirates hunting for treasure on a candy island. The look is bright and playful, but the mechanic that sets it apart sits above the main grid: a short horizontal reel that can turn an ordinary win into a much bigger one.

Wins pay anywhere. Landing 8 or more matching symbols anywhere on the 6x5 grid or the top reel pays, with matching symbols worth up to 100x. Symbols on the top reel can also carry multiplier overlays of up to 500x, and when a win includes several of them, their values are added together and applied to the total win of the spin. Pragmatic Play's announcement describes the same layout as a 6x6 grid, counting the top reel as the sixth row, while the game page describes a 6x5 grid plus the horizontal reel.

The bonus round starts with 4 to 6 bonus symbols, awarding 10 to 30 free spins plus an instant cash prize of up to 2,000x. At least one multiplier overlay is guaranteed on every free spin, and landing 3 bonus symbols during the round adds 5 more free spins. Super bonus symbols can only appear on the top reel: when one forms part of the triggering combination, the round upgrades to Super Free Spins, where every symbol on the top reel carries a multiplier overlay.

The headline numbers are a 96.53% RTP, medium volatility and a maximum win of 10,000x the bet. Pragmatic Play publishes no stake range on the official game page, so check the in-game info for the stakes that apply where you play. All facts on this page come from Pragmatic Play's official Sweet Loot game page and its launch announcement.
