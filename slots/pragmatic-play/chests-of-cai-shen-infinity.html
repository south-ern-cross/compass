Overview

Chests of Cai Shen - Infinity is the third entry in Pragmatic Play's Chests of Cai Shen series, listed by the studio for release on 26 October 2026. The base game keeps the series' misty river valley: pagodas on both banks, drifting boats, and Cai Shen, the god of wealth, among rabbits, pigs and fish on the reels, with 10 to Ace royals below them.

The collection engine is the point of the game. Four coin colours fly up to four chests above the reels: green Prosperity coins, blue Fulfillment coins, red Double coins and purple Longevity coins. Coins land during normal spins, and any chest can crack the respin round open.

The respin round plays like Hold and Win: three respins on a special grid, sticky money symbols worth up to 150x the bet, and every new money symbol resets the counter to three. Each triggering chest adds its own modifier: Prosperity raises the values of up to three money symbols, Fulfillment adds four multiplier zones, Longevity adds a fourth respin, and Double splits the board into two 5x3 grids. Trigger all four chests at once and the Quadruple Bonus runs every modifier together; filling all 15 positions on either grid pays a Grand prize of 5,000x.

The numbers: 96.50% RTP, high volatility, a 15,000x maximum win and stakes from 0.25 to 250. Players who do not want to wait for a chest can buy in where the option is offered: the Fulfillment Bonus costs 100x the bet and the full Quad Bonus costs 750x. Like all Pragmatic Play releases, the game runs in the browser on phones and tablets with no download needed.
