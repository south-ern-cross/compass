Overview

Game of Thrones is Blueprint Gaming's licensed slot built on the HBO series, developed with Warner Bros. Discovery Global Experiences and distributed in North America through White Hat Studios' House of Brands portfolio.

It plays on six reels with 4,096 ways to win, with a persistent Money Collect feature that evolves as players unlock abilities tied to Houses Stark, Lannister, Baratheon and Targaryen.

Landing three or more Bonus symbols triggers the Iron Throne Bonus: 10 Iron Throne Spins with the potential for additional cash prizes, alongside four fixed jackpots ranging from a Mini at 20x to a Mega at 2,500x.

After a record-breaking debut in Canada in April 2026, the game launched in the United States at BetMGM Casino in New Jersey, Michigan, Pennsylvania and West Virginia, and at Borgata Online in New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

Game of Thrones FAQ

What is the RTP and volatility of Game of Thrones?

Blueprint Gaming has not disclosed the RTP or the volatility of Game of Thrones in the official launch materials. The exact figures can differ between casinos, so check the game info or paytable inside the game at the casino where you play.

How does the Money Collect feature work?

The Money Collect feature is persistent across sessions and evolves as players unlock abilities associated with House Stark, House Lannister, House Baratheon and House Targaryen, as they progress on their route towards the Iron Throne.

How do I trigger the Iron Throne Bonus?

Landing three or more Bonus symbols triggers the Iron Throne Bonus, awarding 10 Iron Throne Spins along with the potential for additional cash prizes.

What are the fixed jackpots in Game of Thrones?

Game of Thrones includes four fixed jackpot prizes: Mini at 20x the bet, Minor at 50x, Major at 200x and Mega at 2,500x.
