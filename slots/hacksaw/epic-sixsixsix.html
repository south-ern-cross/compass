Overview

Hacksaw Gaming put its devilish rubber-hose cartoon SixSixSix back through the forge. Epic SixSixSix, released on 22 October 2026, keeps the monochrome underworld and its two pitchfork-waving devils, Balthazar and Beelzebub, but brightens the palette with teal flames and raises the ceiling: the top prize now stands at 20,666x the bet (see FAQ).

Everything revolves around the Wicked Wheels. Blue or Red '6' symbols land on reels 1, 3 and 5 and summon a wheel in the same position; each spin of the wheel can award multipliers, multipliers of multipliers, the max win itself, or, on Blue wheels, another '6' to trigger a fresh wheel. In the base game only Blue wheels appear; Red wheels bring bigger multipliers. Three bonus games wait behind the feature symbols: Speak of the Devil (10 free spins packed with Wicked Wheels), Let Hell Break Loose (10 free spins with all Red wheels) and the Epic Bonus, What the Hell, which guarantees a Red Wicked Wheel on every spin. Impatient players can buy in through Bonushunt FeatureSpins, High-Roller FeatureSpins (where each spin carries a 1 in 11 or 1 in 12 shot at the max win) or the Epic Bonus.

At 5/5 volatility and a 20,666x top prize, this is an aggressive game for players who chase the big swing rather than long sessions. The headline RTP is 96.27%, though casinos may run the lower 94% or 92% settings shown on the official game page, so check the in-game info before you play.

Epic SixSixSix FAQ

What is the RTP of Epic SixSixSix?

The headline RTP of Epic SixSixSix is 96.27%, but Hacksaw Gaming's official game page lists three RTP settings (96%, 94% and 92%) and each casino picks one. The exact figure can differ between casinos, so check the game info or paytable inside the game where you play.

How do the Wicked Wheels work in Epic SixSixSix?

A Blue or Red '6' symbol on reels 1, 3 or 5 summons a Wicked Wheel in the same position. The wheel can award multipliers, multiplying multipliers, the max win, or, on Blue wheels only, another '6' symbol that triggers another wheel. Multiple wheels are collected left to right; only Blue wheels appear in the base game, and Red wheels carry bigger multipliers. Landing one or more '6' symbols on the wheels triggers one of the three bonus games: Speak of the Devil, Let Hell Break Loose or the Epic Bonus, What the Hell.
