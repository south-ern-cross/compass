Overview

Suits & Royals takes players into a Mesoamerican jungle temple in 9 Orbs of Gold, released 14 October 2026 and distributed through Yggdrasil's YGG Champions programme. The game runs on a 5x3 grid with 20 lines, and everything revolves around golden orbs: landing 3 to 9 orbs anywhere pays an instant win of up to 2,000x the bet, and filling the board with all nine orbs triggers the signature full-board hit.

Three scatters on reels 2, 3 and 4 wake the Coin of Fate, which picks one of two bonus rounds (see FAQ). The Hold & Win round locks sticky Gold Coins worth up to 2,000x the bet, while the Free Spins round has no set limit and every win raises the Orb values. Wilds also pay on their own, with five wilds worth up to 2,500x the bet.

Suits & Royals' launch announcement lists a 94% RTP, with low-medium volatility and a 6,010x max multiplier (see FAQ). At a 38% hit frequency, this is a steadier ride than most feature-heavy new releases.
