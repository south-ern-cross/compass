What is M-Pesa

M-Pesa is a mobile money service run by Safaricom in Kenya (with partner networks in other African countries). You keep a balance on your phone number and send money through the SIM menu or app - no bank account needed. It is the default way millions of Kenyans pay for everyday things.

Who M-Pesa is for

M-Pesa is built for players in Kenya, and to a lesser extent Tanzania, the DRC and other markets where the service runs. If you do not have a Kenyan SIM and M-Pesa account, this page is not for you - the method does not work across borders.
