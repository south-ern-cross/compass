What is PIX

PIX is the instant payment system of Brazil, operated by the Central Bank of Brazil. You send money from your bank app using a PIX key - an email address, phone number, tax ID or a random key - and the transfer settles in seconds, 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays. For individuals in Brazil, PIX transfers are free.

Who PIX is for

PIX is built for players in Brazil. If your bank account is Brazilian, PIX is usually the fastest and cheapest way to move money into a casino that supports it - no card fees, no waiting for a bank transfer to clear. Players outside Brazil cannot use PIX, since it requires a Brazilian bank account.

Are there fees for PIX casino deposits?
