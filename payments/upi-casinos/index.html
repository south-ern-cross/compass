What is UPI

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India's instant payment system, run by the National Payments Corporation of India. It links your bank account to payment apps - Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and bank apps - and moves money between accounts in seconds, 24/7. Person-to-person UPI transfers are free.

Who UPI is for

UPI is built for players in India with an Indian bank account. If that is you, UPI is usually the simplest way to fund a casino account that supports it - no card details to type, no waiting. Players outside India cannot use UPI.
