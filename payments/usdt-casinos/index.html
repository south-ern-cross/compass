What is USDT

USDT (Tether) is a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar - one USDT is meant to equal one dollar. Unlike BTC or ETH, its price does not swing day to day, so the amount you deposit is the amount you play with. USDT exists on several blockchains. The ones you will see in casino cashiers are TRC20 (Tron), ERC20 (Ethereum) and Solana. Always use the network the cashier shows: sending USDT on the wrong network can lose the funds.

Who USDT is for

USDT suits any player who wants a dollar balance without touching a bank card - and it is close to essential in Tier-2/3 markets. If your local currency moves against the dollar, or your card gets declined for gambling transactions, USDT bought on a local or peer-to-peer exchange sidesteps both problems. It is the standard bridge currency for players using M-Pesa in Kenya, UPI in India or PIX in Brazil where the casino does not take those methods directly.
