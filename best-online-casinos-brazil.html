FAQ

Can Brazilian players claim crypto casino bonuses?

Offshore crypto casinos accept Brazilian players and their bonuses generally apply. Note that since January 2025 Brazil's regulated market only licenses SPA-authorized operators - offshore casinos sit outside that system.

Can I deposit with PIX and still get the bonus?

At casinos that support PIX, yes - but crypto casinos are crypto-first. Check the cashier for which methods trigger the bonus.

Do bonuses differ for Brazilian players?

Sometimes: casinos may run region-specific promos or exclude some countries from global ones. Read the bonus terms, not just the banner.

What should I check first in a bonus offer?

The wagering multiplier, the max bet while wagering, game weighting and the cashout cap. A 35x requirement with fair terms beats a bigger headline with 60x.
