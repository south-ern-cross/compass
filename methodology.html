Our Methodology

How Spinora Wins evaluates and ranks online casinos. We do not rank purely by commission size - every listed operator is scored against the same weighted criteria below. Total: 100 points (25 + 25 + 20 + 10 + 10 + 10).

1. Licensing & Legal Standing (25% of score)

We verify that an operator holds an active license from a recognized regulator. Our check process:

  • Look up the license number on the regulator's public register (Curaçao GCB, MGA, or national authority)
  • Confirm the license covers casino games (not just sports betting)
  • Check for any public sanctions or warnings against the operator
  • For GEO guides: verify against local authorities (Lagos LSLGA, Coljuegos Colombia, Brazil SPA)

Automatic fail: No verifiable license, expired license, or license from a non-existent "regulator". Such casinos go to our blacklist, not our toplist.

2. Payment Support & Withdrawal Speed (25% of score)

We evaluate deposits and withdrawals based on published terms, user reports, and regulatory records. Our process:

  • Register a real player account (no affiliate or test accounts)
  • Deposit the minimum amount using crypto (BTC/USDT)
  • Play through at least 10 slot spins or 5 table game rounds
  • Request a withdrawal and time it from click to wallet receipt
  • Test live chat during the process with a real question

Scoring: Under 1 hour = full marks. 1-6 hours = good. 6-24 hours = acceptable. Over 24 hours or requires multiple support contacts = scored down. Withdrawal refused or stalled = automatic blacklist.

3. Customer Support Quality (10% of score)

We evaluate support based on:

  • Live chat response time (under 2 minutes = full marks)
  • Quality of answers (specific vs. copy-paste)
  • Available channels (live chat, email, phone)
  • Support hours (24/7 vs. limited)

4. Bonus Fairness (20% of score)

We read the full bonus terms, not just the headline. For each bonus we record:

  • Wagering multiplier (e.g. 35x) and whether it applies to bonus only or deposit+bonus
  • Maximum bet allowed while wagering (exceeding it can void winnings)
  • Game weighting (slots often 100%, table games 10% or less)
  • Maximum cashout cap on bonus winnings
  • Time limit to complete wagering

We calculate the true cost using our Wagering Calculator. A $100 bonus at 50x wagering costs $5,000 in bets. Bonuses with no wagering (like Cloudbet's cash rewards) score highest.

5. Game Library & Software Providers (10% of score)

We verify the actual game catalog, not marketing claims:

  • Count of real-money slots, table games, and live dealer tables
  • Presence of top providers (Pragmatic Play, Evolution, NetEnt)
  • RTP data cross-checked against our Slot RTP Database
  • Mobile compatibility (HTML5, no Flash relics)

6. Ongoing Re-Verification (10% of score)

Scores decay without maintenance. We track:

  • Last bonus terms check date (bonuses change monthly)
  • License status re-verified quarterly
  • Withdrawal speed re-tested after major complaints
  • Player complaint volume monitored via our complaints page

Casinos that stop responding to player complaints or change terms unfavorably lose points here.
