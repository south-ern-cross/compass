ETH Tips Before You Deposit

Match the network. The cashier shows a deposit address with a network label - send only on that network. Funds sent over the wrong network may not be credited - always match the network the casino shows.

Budget for gas. You pay a gas fee when sending ETH from your wallet to the casino. Depositing larger amounts less often beats many small deposits.

Consider stablecoins for the bankroll. If you want to avoid price swings between sessions, consider funding play in a stablecoin like USDT or USDC - but check the casino's cashier for which coins are currently supported.

FAQ

What is an Ethereum casino?

An Ethereum casino is an online casino where you can deposit, play and withdraw using ETH. Ethereum transactions are often faster and cheaper than Bitcoin, but it depends on network congestion and fees at the time, which makes ETH a popular choice for casino deposits and withdrawals.

Do I pay gas fees when depositing ETH at a casino?

You pay a gas fee when you send ETH from your own wallet to the casino's deposit address - that fee goes to the Ethereum network, not the casino. Withdrawal terms differ by casino, so check the cashier for network fees before you cash out.

Is ETH faster than BTC for casino withdrawals?

Often yes. Ethereum blocks confirm roughly every 12 seconds, while Bitcoin blocks take about 10 minutes, so ETH withdrawals usually reach your wallet sooner once the casino processes them. Both are much faster than fiat bank withdrawals.

Which network should I use to send ETH to a casino?

Always match the network the casino's cashier shows on the deposit address. If the cashier gives you an Ethereum mainnet address, send only via Ethereum mainnet - sending via a different network can lose your funds.

Do welcome bonuses apply to Ethereum deposits?

At the casinos ranked here, bonuses are denominated in the deposit currency - check the casino's bonus T&Cs for how they apply to ETH deposits. Always confirm the current bonus terms on the casino's site before depositing.
