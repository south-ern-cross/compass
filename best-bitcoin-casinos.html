FAQ

What is a Bitcoin casino?

A Bitcoin casino is an online casino where you deposit, wager and withdraw using Bitcoin. It works like a regular online casino, except the currency is BTC. BitStarz, for example, states deposits and withdrawals settle in about 10 minutes on average - actual speed varies by casino, coin and network congestion.

How do I deposit Bitcoin at an online casino?

Create an account, open the cashier, select Bitcoin as the deposit method, and copy the casino's BTC deposit address into your wallet app. Send the amount you want to play with. Funds are credited after the transaction is confirmed on the Bitcoin network, usually within 10-20 minutes.

Are Bitcoin casino withdrawals faster than fiat?

Yes, in most cases. Bitcoin withdrawals skip the banking system, so once the casino processes your request, the coins reach your wallet after one network confirmation cycle - often under an hour. Fiat withdrawals can take 1-5 business days.

Do welcome bonuses apply to Bitcoin deposits?

At most Bitcoin-first casinos, yes - bonuses are denominated in BTC or its fiat equivalent and apply equally to crypto deposits. Always check the bonus terms on the casino's site, because some operators exclude certain payment methods from bonus eligibility.

What is the wagering requirement on the BitStarz welcome bonus?

The BitStarz welcome package of up to 5 BTC + 180 free spins carries 40x wagering on the bonus amount. Always confirm the current terms on the BitStarz site before depositing.
