Casino Bonuses in Brazil: What the Law Allows

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

Home / Brazil / Bonuses Guide
TL;DR: Licensed Brazilian casinos cannot offer welcome bonuses, free bets, or any advantage as an inducement to bet - the law forbids it outright. Offshore sites still advertise bonuses to Brazilians, but those sites are unlicensed. If you take a bonus anywhere, learn to read the wagering terms first.

What the Law Actually Says

Law 14,790 is blunt: "It is forbidden for the operating agent to grant, in any form, advancement, anticipation, bonus, or previous advantage, even if merely for promotion, dissemination, or advertising, for placing bets." In plain language: a licensed operator cannot give you anything extra to get you to bet. No welcome bonus, no free bet on signup, no deposit match as a sign-up incentive.

This is deliberate policy. The government wants betting to be a straight transaction - you deposit, you bet, you win or lose - without promotional mechanics pulling people in.

What This Means on Licensed Sites

  • No welcome offers. The signup flow is KYC, deposit, play. Nothing extra for joining.
  • No bonus advertising. SPA advertising rules (Portaria 1,231/2024) tightly control marketing: ads must include responsible gambling warnings and 18+ notices, and cannot present betting as income or target minors.
  • Loyalty programs are constrained. Anything that functions as an inducement to bet more falls under the ban. Licensed operators stay conservative here.

Offshore Sites Still Dangle Bonuses

Unlicensed offshore casinos aggressively market bonuses to Brazilians: 100% deposit matches, free spins, cashback. These sites are outside Brazilian regulation, and their bonus terms are often designed to be hard to clear. Common traps:

  • High wagering: 35x-50x the bonus amount is typical offshore; some go higher.
  • Max-bet rules: betting over a small cap (often around R$25 equivalent) while wagering can void your winnings. This is the most common reason players lose bonus winnings in disputes.
  • Game restrictions: many games contribute 0-10% toward wagering.
  • Time limits: 7-30 days to clear, or the bonus and winnings vanish.
  • Max cashout: some bonuses cap what you can withdraw from bonus winnings.

How to Read Any Bonus Terms (60 Seconds)

  1. Find the wagering requirement (e.g. 35x) and what it applies to (bonus only, or deposit + bonus).
  2. Find the max bet while wagering. Never exceed it.
  3. Check game contributions and the time limit.
  4. Check max cashout on bonus winnings.
  5. Screenshot the terms. Operators change them; your screenshot is your evidence in a dispute.

FAQ

Why can't I find welcome bonuses at Brazilian casinos?

Law 14,790 forbids operators from granting, in any form, bonuses or advantages as an inducement to place bets. Licensed sites comply with this; the absence of bonus offers is a sign of a legal operator, not a worse one.

Are all bonuses illegal in Brazil?

The ban targets bonuses used as an inducement to bet or as marketing. Licensed operators operate without sign-up incentives. Offshore sites are outside this framework entirely.

What about cashback or VIP programs?

Licensed operators must stay within the law's ban on advantages granted to induce betting. Treat any promotional offer from a .bet.br site with skepticism and read the SPA's current guidance - and ours below on reading terms.

Can offshore sites offer me bonuses?

They do, but they are not licensed in Brazil. Bonus terms on offshore sites can be harsh: high wagering, max-bet rules, and game restrictions. Read every term before depositing.

What is wagering, in simple terms?

Wagering (rollover) is how many times you must bet the bonus amount before withdrawing winnings from it. A R$100 bonus with 35x wagering means R$3,500 in bets before cashout. Our wagering calculator does the math for you.
