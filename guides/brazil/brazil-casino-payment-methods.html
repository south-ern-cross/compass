Casino Payment Methods in Brazil (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

  1. Home

  3. Payment Methods
TL;DR: On SPA-licensed casinos, the payment menu is set by law: PIX, TED, debit cards, prepaid cards, and bank transfers only. Credit cards, boleto, cash, cheques, and crypto are banned. Offshore casinos play by different rules, which is exactly why they are riskier for Brazilian players.

The Legal Payment Menu (Licensed Sites)

SPA Normative Ordinance 615/2024 sets the payment framework for the regulated market. The goal is a closed, traceable loop: money moves only between the player's registered bank account and the operator's account, both at institutions authorized by the Central Bank of Brazil.

Allowed: PIX, TED (electronic transfer), debit cards, prepaid cards, and bank transfers authorized by the Central Bank. Payment providers used by operators must themselves be registered with the Central Bank.

Banned: credit cards, cash, boleto (payment slips), cheques, and virtual assets such as cryptocurrencies. No third-party accounts: deposits must come from an account in the player's own name.

PIX: The Standard

PIX is the Central Bank's instant payment system, launched in 2020, and it handles the vast majority of legal betting transactions. Transfers settle in seconds, 24/7, including nights and holidays. You pay using a PIX key (CPF, phone, email, or random key) or by scanning a QR code in the casino cashier.

  • Deposits: typically credited instantly. Minimum deposits are set by each operator, commonly in the R$10-50 range.
  • Withdrawals: the law requires prize payment within 120 minutes of the event ending; PIX itself adds only seconds.
  • Fees: usually zero for individuals.

Our dedicated PIX guide goes deeper on limits, timing, and fixing failed transfers.

TED and Bank Transfers

TED (Transferência Eletrônica Disponível) and other authorized bank transfers work as a fallback. They are slower than PIX on weekends and holidays, and some banks charge fees. In practice, if a casino offers PIX, there is little reason to use TED.

Debit and Prepaid Cards

Debit cards linked to your own bank account are allowed. Prepaid cards are useful if you want a hard spending cap: load a fixed amount and you cannot overspend. Credit cards, even debit-function credit cards used as credit, are not allowed.

Why Crypto and Boleto Are Banned

The regulator's logic is traceability and harm reduction. Crypto obscures the money trail and complicates anti-money-laundering checks. Boletos and cash break the closed loop between registered accounts. Credit cards let players gamble with borrowed money, which the government explicitly wants to prevent.

Offshore Casinos: Different Rules, More Risk

Unlicensed offshore casinos serving Brazilians commonly accept crypto, credit cards, and e-wallets. None of this is covered by Brazilian regulation, and the SPA has ordered thousands of such sites blocked. Payouts depend entirely on the operator's goodwill. If fast, safe withdrawals matter to you, the licensed market with PIX is the better deal despite the lack of bonuses.

Withdrawal Checklist

  • Complete KYC (CPF + facial recognition) before your first withdrawal to avoid delays.
  • Withdraw to the same account you deposited from - the closed-loop rule requires it.
  • If a licensed operator misses the 120-minute payout window, document it and use our complaints process.

FAQ

Can I deposit at a licensed casino with a credit card?

No. SPA rules ban all credit card transactions on licensed operators, including through payment providers. Use PIX, a debit card, or a prepaid card instead.

Can I use boleto to fund a betting account?

No. Payment slips (boletos) are banned on licensed sites, along with cash, cheques, and crypto. All transactions must be electronic transfers between registered bank accounts.

Can I use crypto at a licensed Brazilian casino?

No. Virtual assets including cryptocurrencies are prohibited on licensed sites. Crypto casinos serving Brazilians operate offshore, outside Brazilian regulation.

How long do withdrawals take?

Licensed operators must pay prizes within 120 minutes of the event ending. PIX transfers themselves settle in seconds once released.

Are there fees for PIX deposits?

PIX transfers are free or near-free for individuals at most banks. Casinos may set their own minimum deposit amounts, so check the cashier page.
