Casino Bonuses for Indian Players: Wagering Explained

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

  1. Home
  2. India
  3. Bonuses Guide
TL;DR: A bonus is only as good as its terms. The wagering requirement tells you how much you must bet before withdrawing; the max-bet rule caps your stake per spin while wagering; game restrictions decide which games count. In India there is one extra catch: bonus winnings are taxed at a flat 30%. Learn these numbers before accepting any bonus - they matter more than the bonus size.

Types of Bonuses You Will See

  • Welcome bonus: extra money on your first deposit(s), usually a percentage match. The most common offer.
  • Free spins: a set number of spins on specific slots. Winnings are usually paid as bonus money with wagering.
  • No-deposit bonus: a small amount just for registering. Rare, and usually comes with high wagering and low max cashout.
  • Reload bonus: a match on later deposits, for existing players.
  • Cashback: a percentage of net losses returned, sometimes with no wagering - often the fairest type.

We do not list specific bonus amounts here because they change constantly. Check the current offer on the operator's site - and then read the terms below before you claim it.

Wagering Requirements: The Math

The wagering (or playthrough) requirement is the multiplier on how much you must bet before bonus funds become withdrawable. Example:

  • You deposit Rs. 10,000 and get a Rs. 10,000 bonus with 35x wagering on the bonus.
  • You must place Rs. 350,000 in qualifying bets before withdrawing.
  • On slots with 96% RTP, the expected cost of that wagering is roughly 4% of 350,000 = Rs. 14,000 - more than the bonus itself.

Use our wagering calculator and bonus EV calculator to run the numbers on any offer.

As a rough guide: 20-30x on slots is reasonable; 35-40x is average; 50x and above is poor value for most players.

The Max-Bet Rule: The Biggest Trap

Almost every bonus caps your stake while wagering - commonly a few hundred rupees per spin or round (the exact figure is in the terms). Break it once, even accidentally, and the casino can void the bonus and all winnings from it. This is the single most common reason players lose bonus winnings in disputes.

Protect yourself: note the max bet before you start, and do not use autoplay at high stakes while a bonus is active.

Game Restrictions

  • Slots: usually count 100% toward wagering.
  • Table games (blackjack, roulette, baccarat): often 5-10% or excluded.
  • Live casino: frequently excluded entirely.
  • High-RTP slots: some casinos exclude specific slots (often those above 97-98% RTP) from bonus wagering.

Always check the contribution table in the bonus terms. Playing an excluded game can void the bonus just like breaking the max-bet rule.

The Indian Tax Catch

Winnings from bonus play are still winnings. Under Section 115BBJ of the Income Tax Act, gambling winnings are taxed at a flat 30% (plus surcharge and cess), with 30% TDS under Section 194BA on net winnings (no threshold; offshore casinos typically do not withhold, so self-reporting is the player's responsibility). That applies to what you win from a bonus just as it applies to wins from your own deposits.

Practical example: you clear a bonus and withdraw Rs. 50,000 in winnings. Expect 30% TDS - Rs. 15,000 - to be deducted, leaving you Rs. 35,000. The bonus math already looked thin; the tax makes it thinner. We are not tax advisors.

Other Terms That Matter

  • Expiry: bonuses typically expire in 7-30 days. Unfinished wagering means the bonus and its winnings disappear.
  • Max cashout: some bonuses (especially no-deposit ones) cap how much you can win.
  • Payment exclusions: deposits by certain methods (sometimes e-wallets or crypto) may not qualify for the bonus.
  • One per household: claiming the same welcome bonus twice (multiple accounts) is bonus abuse and gets winnings confiscated.

Should You Take the Bonus?

Honest answer: often no. If you want to play freely, withdraw whenever you like, and bet any stake, skip the bonus. Your deposit has no wagering and no max-bet rule without it.

Take the bonus when: wagering is 30x or lower, the max bet is comfortable for your play style, your favorite games count 100%, and the expiry gives you enough time. Compare offers with our bonus comparator.

Disputes start here. Most bonus disputes we see come from players who did not read the max-bet rule or the game restrictions. Five minutes with the terms saves weeks of complaints. Our complaints guide shows what happens when it goes wrong.

FAQ

What is a wagering requirement?

A wagering requirement tells you how much you must bet before bonus money becomes withdrawable. A 35x requirement on a Rs. 10,000 bonus means you must place Rs. 350,000 in bets before you can withdraw the bonus or winnings from it.

What is the max bet rule?

Most bonuses cap how much you can bet per spin or round while wagering. Bet more than the cap, even once, and the casino can void your bonus and winnings. This is the most common reason players lose bonus winnings.

Do all games count the same toward wagering?

No. Slots usually count 100 percent. Table games like blackjack and roulette often count 5-10 percent or are excluded entirely. Live casino games are frequently excluded. Check the bonus terms for the contribution table.

Do I pay tax on bonus winnings in India?

Winnings from gambling, including winnings from bonus play, are taxed at a flat 30% (plus surcharge and cess) under Section 115BBJ with 30% TDS under Section 194BA on net winnings (no threshold; offshore casinos typically do not withhold, so self-reporting is the player's responsibility). The tax applies to what you win, not to the bonus itself. We are not tax advisors.

Are casino bonuses worth it?

Sometimes. A bonus with 20-30x wagering on slots can be decent value. A bonus with 50x+ wagering, short expiry, and heavy game restrictions usually is not. Use our wagering calculator to see what a bonus really costs you.

Can I refuse a casino bonus?

Yes. Most casinos let you opt out at deposit, or you can ask support to remove a bonus before you play. Playing without a bonus means no wagering requirements and no max-bet rule - your money is yours to withdraw anytime.
