Casino Payment Methods for Indian Players (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

TL;DR: UPI is the default for Indian casino players: instant, works with any bank, typically Rs. 300 to Rs. 100,000 per deposit. Paytm and PhonePe run on the same rails. NetBanking suits larger amounts. Crypto operates outside the banking system. Since the PROGA Act 2025, banks must block gambling transactions, so expect occasional declines and be prepared for declines ready.

UPI: The Default Option

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India's real-time payment system, run by NPCI, and it is the most practical way to fund a casino account from India. Every major bank supports it, and apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM all ride the same rails.

  • Typical deposit range: around Rs. 300 minimum to Rs. 100,000 maximum per transaction.
  • Speed: usually instant to 10 minutes.
  • How it works: the casino shows a QR code or UPI ID; you scan or enter it in your UPI app and confirm with your PIN.
  • Withdrawals: some casinos pay out via UPI, but many do not. Check before depositing.

The catch: under the PROGA Act 2025, banks and payment systems are barred from processing gambling transactions. In practice, enforcement is uneven - many UPI deposits go through, but some banks decline them. A declined UPI payment is almost always the bank's doing, not the casino's.

Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay

These apps are mostly UPI front-ends, so the mechanics are identical to UPI. The differences are cosmetic: selecting PhonePe or Google Pay in a casino cashier usually shows a QR code or deep link that opens directly in that app.

  • Paytm: deposits typically Rs. 300 to Rs. 50,000, processed in about 5-15 minutes. At many casinos Paytm is deposit-only - withdrawals go through UPI, bank transfer, or another method. Anyone with a Paytm wallet can also use the Paytm UPI option.
  • PhonePe / Google Pay: same UPI ranges as above. Note that Google Pay displays the account holder name during payment, so the casino account name and the payer name should match.

NetBanking

NetBanking (including IMPS) needs no third-party app - just a bank account with internet banking enabled. SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and Kotak Mahindra are the banks most commonly supported in casino cashiers.

  • Typical range: around Rs. 500 minimum, up to several lakh maximum.
  • Speed: instant to about 30 minutes.
  • Best for: larger deposits where UPI limits get in the way.

Credit and Debit Cards

Visa and Mastercard deposits are hit-or-miss for Indian players. Many Indian banks decline gambling-coded card transactions automatically. Cards are worth trying as a backup, but do not rely on them as your primary method.

E-wallets: Skrill and Neteller

Skrill and Neteller are widely accepted at offshore casinos and work well for both deposits and withdrawals. The trade-off is an extra account to fund and verify, plus e-wallet fees on some transfers. They are a solid middle ground between UPI and crypto.

Crypto: Bypassing the Banking System

Crypto deposits sidestep banks entirely, which is why they are popular with Indian players since the 2025 ban. Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT are the standard options. Some casinos, like Stake, are crypto-only: no UPI, no NetBanking, just crypto.

The practical workflow: buy crypto on a KYC-verified Indian exchange (CoinDCX and similar major Indian exchanges handle INR conversion (we do not recommend specific exchanges)), then transfer it to the casino's wallet address. Blockchain confirmations take roughly 10-20 minutes for Ethereum and USDT, longer for Bitcoin. Withdrawals in crypto are usually the fastest payout option available.

FAQ

Can I use UPI at online casinos?

Yes, most offshore casinos targeting India accept UPI deposits via QR code or UPI ID, typically from around Rs. 300 up to Rs. 100,000. Deposits are usually instant. Note that banks are barred from processing gambling transactions under the PROGA Act 2025, so deposits can be blocked.

Does Paytm work for casino deposits?

Paytm works at some casinos, typically with deposits from around Rs. 300 to Rs. 50,000. At many casinos Paytm is deposit-only: withdrawals must go through UPI, bank transfer, or another method. Anyone with a Paytm wallet can also pay via the Paytm UPI option.

Can I deposit with crypto from India?

Yes. Crypto-only casinos accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT with no banking involved. Indian players typically buy crypto on a KYC-verified domestic exchange such as WazirX, CoinDCX, or Mudrex, then transfer it to the casino wallet.

Why was my UPI deposit declined?

Most declines come from the bank, not the casino. Under the PROGA Act 2025, banks and payment systems are barred from processing gambling transactions, and some enforce this strictly. Deposits may be declined; we cannot advise on bypassing bank controls.

How do casino withdrawals work for Indian players?

Withdrawals usually go to bank accounts via UPI or wire transfer, to e-wallets like Skrill or Neteller, or in crypto. First withdrawals almost always require KYC. Check the cashier's withdrawal options and minimums before you deposit.

Are there fees on casino deposits?

Most casinos charge no platform fee on deposits. Your bank or UPI provider may apply its own standard charges, and crypto deposits carry network fees. Currency conversion fees apply if you play in USD or EUR instead of INR.
