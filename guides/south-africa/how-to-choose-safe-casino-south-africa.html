How to Check If an Online Casino Is Safe in South Africa (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

  1. Home
  2. South Africa
  3. How to Choose a Safe Casino
TL;DR: No South African online casino license exists, so ignore license claims and check the offshore license on the regulator's own register instead. Verify KYC, read the withdrawal and bonus terms before depositing, set limits, and remember: no SA body can force an offshore casino to pay you.

Step 1: Understand the Legal Starting Point

There is no South African online casino license to check for. Provincial licenses (WCGRB, Gauteng Gambling Board and others) cover sports betting bookmakers, not online casinos. Anyone claiming to hold a "South African online casino license" is lying. Start from there, and the rest of the checklist makes sense.

Step 2: Verify the Offshore License

A serious offshore casino holds a license somewhere real. The two you will see most:

  • Malta (MGA): search the operator name or domain on the Malta Gaming Authority register. It should show the company, the license number and its status.
  • Curacao (GCB): check the license seal against the Gaming Control Board portal. Curacao licenses are common and weaker than MGA, but a verifiable one beats an unverifiable claim.

Ignore the logo, trust the register. Scam sites display copied MGA and Curacao seals routinely.

Step 3: Read the Terms Before Depositing

Read the withdrawal rules, not the bonus banner. Check:

  • Withdrawal timeframes and limits (see our payments guide).
  • Bonus wagering requirements, max-bet rules and game restrictions (see our bonuses guide).
  • KYC requirements: what documents, and when.
  • Account dormancy and closure rules.

If the terms are only in a language you cannot read, or the support agent cannot explain the bonus rules, walk away.

Step 4: Check Responsible-Gambling Tools

Before depositing, confirm the casino offers deposit limits you can set yourself, session reminders, and self-exclusion. If none exist, do not play there.

Red Flags: Walk Away Immediately

  • Claims of a South African casino license. It does not exist.
  • Pressure to deposit via crypto, gift cards or P2P only. Serious casinos offer normal rails too (Ozow, cards, vouchers).
  • No KYC. A casino that pays large withdrawals with zero verification is not compliant with anything.
  • Hidden bonus terms. If wagering requirements cannot be found, assume the worst.
  • Unrealistic promises. "Guaranteed wins", "no wagering ever on everything", pressure to deposit now.

If Something Goes Wrong

At an offshore casino: complain to the operator in writing (email or chat transcript), keep every record, and escalate to the licensing authority if unresolved. Our complaints guide walks through the process. Understand the limit: no South African body can force an offshore casino to pay you. This is the main reason to be strict about steps 1-4 before you deposit a rand.

Play responsibly: 18+ only. Set deposit limits, take breaks, and use self-exclusion tools if gambling stops being fun. National Responsible Gambling helpline: 0800 006 008. Learn more.

FAQ

Are there licensed online casinos in South Africa?

No domestic online casino licenses exist. The National Gambling Act 2004 prohibits interactive games, and no operator holds a South African license for online slots, roulette or poker. Only provincial bookmaker licenses exist, and they cover sports betting. Every online casino serving SA players is offshore.

How do I verify an offshore license?

Malta (MGA): search the operator or domain on the Malta Gaming Authority's own register. Curacao: check the Gaming Control Board portal for the license seal. A logo in the casino's footer proves nothing - anyone can copy an image. Only the regulator's own database counts.

What are red flags of an unsafe casino?

Fake or unverifiable license claims, no terms and conditions you can actually read, no identity verification before withdrawals, pressure to deposit only in crypto or gift cards, no responsible-gambling tools, and bonus terms that are hidden or impossible to find.

What should I do before my first deposit?

Verify the license on the regulator's own site, complete KYC identity verification, read the withdrawal rules and bonus terms (wagering, max bet, game restrictions), and set deposit limits in your account before playing.

Where can I complain about an offshore casino?

Complain to the casino in writing first and keep records. If unresolved, escalate to the authority that issued its license (MGA, Curacao GCB). There is no South African regulator for offshore casino disputes - that is the real cost of playing offshore. Our complaints guide walks through the process.
