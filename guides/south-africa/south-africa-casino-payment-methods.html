Casino Payment Methods for South Africa (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

Home / South Africa / Payment Methods
TL;DR: Ozow instant EFT is the default deposit rail for SA players, prepaid vouchers work without a bank account, cards sometimes get declined by banks, and crypto is the fastest withdrawal option at crypto-friendly brands. Vouchers are deposit-only - winnings always leave via bank EFT or another payout method.

Ozow (Instant EFT): The Default

Ozow is the most widely used deposit method among SA-facing casinos. It is an instant bank transfer: you select your bank from a list, authenticate through your existing online banking interface, and the funds reflect in the casino wallet within seconds. No card details are shared with the casino. Supported banks include Capitec, FNB, Standard Bank, ABSA, Nedbank, TymeBank and Discovery Bank, and Ozow reports more than six million South African users.

Two alternatives work the same way: SiD and EasyEFT, both secure instant EFT rails for SA banks. A minority of casinos also route withdrawals back through instant EFT rails (15 minutes to 2 hours after casino approval); most pay out via standard bank EFT instead.

Prepaid Vouchers: 1Voucher, OTT, BluVoucher

Vouchers are the answer for players without online banking or who prefer not to connect a bank account. 1Voucher is sold at more than 75,000 retail locations - Pick n Pay, Checkers, Shoprite and most petrol stations - in values from R10 to R4,000. You buy the voucher for cash, enter the 16-digit PIN at the casino cashier, and play. Important limitation: SA anti-money-laundering rules generally prohibit voucher withdrawals, so winnings must be paid out via bank EFT. This is a regulatory requirement, not a casino policy.

Cards: Visa and Mastercard

Cards are accepted at many offshore casinos and deposits are instant. The catch: South African banks sometimes decline or flag transactions they identify as gambling-related. If your card is declined, instant EFT is usually the reliable fallback. Never share card details over chat or email - only enter them in the casino's secure cashier.

E-wallets: Skrill, Neteller, ecoPayz

E-wallets are mainly an offshore-casino option: fast deposits, an extra layer of privacy between your bank and the casino, and support at many international brands. They require a separate account and sometimes carry their own fees on withdrawals.

SnapScan and Zapper

QR-based mobile payments familiar from SA retail. A few casinos and bookmakers accept SnapScan: you scan a QR code with the app and approve the payment on your phone. Fast and mobile-friendly.

Crypto: BTC, ETH, USDT, LTC

Crypto-friendly offshore brands accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT and Litecoin. Deposits confirm on-chain (minutes), withdrawals are usually the fastest option available, and there are no bank rails to block the transaction. Downsides: price volatility between deposit and withdrawal, and no chargeback mechanism. USDT avoids the volatility but not the irreversibility. Our crypto payments guide covers the mechanics.

Every serious casino verifies identity before paying out (FICA-style KYC): ID document, proof of address, sometimes proof of payment method. Complete verification before you deposit, not when you are waiting on a big win - it is the single biggest cause of withdrawal delays. Keep records of every transaction.

FAQ

What is the most popular deposit method for SA players?

Ozow instant EFT: you pick your bank, log in through your own online banking, and the casino is credited instantly without your card details being shared. Supported banks include Capitec, FNB, Standard Bank, ABSA, Nedbank, TymeBank and Discovery Bank.

Can I use prepaid vouchers?

Yes. 1Voucher, OTT Voucher and BluVoucher are sold at more than 75,000 retail locations including Pick n Pay, Checkers and Shoprite, in values from R10 to R4,000. You enter a PIN at the cashier. Vouchers are deposit-only: winnings are paid out via bank EFT.

Do South African banks block gambling transactions?

Some banks flag or decline card transactions they identify as gambling-related. Instant EFT through your bank's own online banking (Ozow, SiD, EasyEFT) is generally more reliable than cards for SA players.

Can I deposit with crypto?

At crypto-friendly offshore brands, yes: BTC, ETH, USDT and LTC are the common options. Crypto bypasses bank rails entirely but carries price volatility and no chargeback if something goes wrong.

How long do withdrawals take?

Bank EFT withdrawals typically take 1-3 business days after the casino approves the request. A few casinos support instant EFT withdrawals that arrive within hours. Identity verification (FICA-style KYC) is required before the first payout.
