Wagering Requirements in Plain Language

A wagering requirement (also called playthrough or rollover) is the number of times you must bet the bonus amount before you can withdraw any winnings from it.

Example: you get a 100,000 COP bonus with 30x wagering. You must place 3,000,000 COP in total bets (30 x 100,000) before the bonus winnings become withdrawable cash. This does not mean losing 3,000,000 COP - wins and losses both count toward the total, but you need that volume of betting.

The Five Terms That Actually Matter

  • Wagering multiplier. Lower is better. 20x-35x is typical for Colombian licensed casinos. Anything above 50x is hard to clear.
  • Game weighting. Slots usually count 100% toward wagering. Table games like blackjack and roulette often count 10% or less, or are excluded entirely.
  • Maximum bet while wagering. Most bonuses cap your bet size (for example 10,000 COP per spin) while wagering is active. Exceeding it can void your winnings - this is the most common reason bonuses are confiscated.
  • Time limit. Usually 7-30 days to complete wagering. Miss the deadline and the bonus plus winnings disappear.
  • Maximum convertible winnings. Some bonuses cap how much you can actually withdraw from bonus winnings, no matter how much you win.

How the 2026 Deposit Tax Affects Bonuses

Since March 2026, licensed operators collect a 16% consumption tax on deposits at the moment of deposit. This matters for bonuses in two ways:

  • If a bonus is "100% up to 200,000 COP", check whether it is calculated on your gross deposit or on the post-tax playable balance. A 200,000 COP deposit leaves roughly 168,000 COP playable after tax - and the bonus math may follow the smaller number.
  • Your effective cost per bonus peso is higher than before the tax. A bonus that was marginal at 0% tax can become bad value at 16%.

Always read the current bonus terms on the casino's own promotions page. We do not publish specific bonus amounts here because they change constantly - any site quoting exact 2026 bonus figures without a date is likely outdated.

Types of Bonuses at Colombian Casinos

  • Welcome bonus: a match on your first deposit(s). The most common offer.
  • Free spins: a set number of spins on specific slots. Winnings usually carry wagering requirements.
  • Free bets: common at sportsbooks - a free wager of a fixed amount.
  • Cashback: a percentage of net losses returned, often with low or no wagering. Usually the fairest type.
  • Reload bonuses: smaller matches on later deposits for existing players.

When to Decline a Bonus

You are never required to accept a bonus. Consider declining when:

  • The wagering requirement is above 40x.
  • You mainly play table games (they barely count toward wagering).
  • You want to withdraw winnings quickly with no restrictions.
  • The maximum bet cap is lower than your normal bet size.

At licensed casinos you can usually opt out during deposit or in account settings. Playing with your own money and no strings is often the better deal.

FAQ

What is a wagering requirement?

A wagering requirement tells you how many times you must bet the bonus amount before you can withdraw winnings from it. A 100,000 COP bonus with 30x wagering means you must place 3,000,000 COP in bets before cashing out.

Do Colombian casinos offer welcome bonuses?

Yes, most Coljuegos-licensed operators offer welcome bonuses, free spins, or free bets. The exact offers change frequently, so always check the casino's promotions page for current terms rather than relying on old reviews.

How does the 2026 deposit tax affect bonuses?

Since March 2026, licensed operators collect a 16% consumption tax on deposits. If a bonus is calculated as a percentage of your deposit, the bonus may be based on the post-tax playable amount, not the gross deposit. Check the bonus terms.

What bonus terms should I always read?

Wagering requirement (the multiplier), game weighting (slots usually count 100%, table games less), maximum bet while wagering, time limit to complete wagering, and maximum convertible winnings.

Can I refuse a casino bonus?

Yes. At licensed casinos you can usually opt out of a bonus during deposit or in your account settings. If you prefer to play with no wagering strings attached, declining the bonus is often the smarter choice.
