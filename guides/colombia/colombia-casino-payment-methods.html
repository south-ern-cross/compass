Casino Payment Methods in Colombia (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

  1. Home
  2. Colombia
  3. Payment Methods
TL;DR: Colombian players have PSE (bank transfer, the standard), Nequi and Daviplata (mobile wallets), Efecty (cash vouchers), credit/debit cards, and crypto at international sites. PSE is the default at Coljuegos-licensed casinos. Since March 2026, a 16% consumption tax applies to deposits at licensed platforms.

PSE: The Standard

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Linea) is Colombia's online bank-transfer system and the default deposit method at every Coljuegos-licensed casino. You select PSE at the cashier, choose your bank, authenticate on your bank's own website, and approve the transfer. Deposits are usually near-instant.

  • Speed: near-instant deposits.
  • Typical limits: around 10,000 - 20,000 COP minimum, up to 10,000,000 COP per transaction.
  • Fees: PSE itself typically charges no player fee.
  • Banks: Bancolombia, Banco de Bogota, Davivienda, Banco de Occidente, BBVA Colombia, and most others with online banking.

Full breakdown: PSE payment method guide.

Nequi: The Mobile Wallet

Nequi is a digital wallet backed by Bancolombia, hugely popular in Colombia for everyday payments and now for casino deposits too. Many licensed casinos accept it. You approve the deposit in the Nequi app on your phone - no bank login needed at the casino.

  • Speed: instant deposits.
  • Typical limits: around 10,000 COP minimum, up to 4,000,000 COP.
  • Fees: usually none from Nequi.
  • Withdrawals: some casinos support Nequi withdrawals, not all. Check the cashier first.

Daviplata: The Other Big Wallet

Daviplata (by Davivienda) is Colombia's other major mobile wallet. Acceptance at casinos is growing but less universal than Nequi. It works the same way: approve in the app, money moves instantly.

Efecty: Cash Without a Bank Account

Efecty operates thousands of payment points across Colombia. You walk in, pay cash, get a voucher code, and redeem it at the casino cashier. It is the best option if you do not use online banking or prefer not to link a bank account.

  • Speed: voucher is issued immediately; redeeming online takes minutes.
  • Withdrawals: Efecty is deposit-only. Winnings must be withdrawn another way.
  • Fees: Efecty points may charge a small service fee on the cash payment.

Credit and Debit Cards

Visa and Mastercard are accepted at most licensed casinos. Deposits are instant. Two caveats:

  • Some Colombian banks block gambling transactions, especially to merchants they do not recognize. If your card is declined, PSE from the same bank usually works.
  • Card withdrawals typically take 3-5 business days, slower than PSE or wallet withdrawals.

Crypto: Only at International Sites

Coljuegos-licensed operators generally do not accept cryptocurrency. International crypto casinos (Bitcoin, USDT) do accept Colombian players, but they are not licensed by Coljuegos - you get fast, private payments but lose Colombian regulatory protection. See our top 10 crypto casinos if you go this route, and understand the tradeoff.

The 16% Deposit Tax

Since March 2026, licensed operators must collect a 16% consumption tax on deposits to online gambling platforms, at the moment of deposit. Example: you deposit 100,000 COP, about 16,000 COP goes to tax, and roughly 84,000 COP reaches your playable balance. The casino should show this breakdown before you confirm. International unlicensed sites do not collect this tax - which is one reason some players drift offshore, and exactly why Coljuegos blocks those sites.

Withdrawals: What to Expect

  • PSE / bank transfer: usually the fastest licensed-casino withdrawal, often within 24 hours after KYC approval.
  • Nequi: fast where supported, sometimes same-day.
  • Cards: 3-5 business days is normal.
  • First withdrawal: always slower - the casino must verify your cedula and address (KYC). Complete verification before you deposit to avoid delays later.

FAQ

What is the most popular casino payment method in Colombia?

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Linea) is the standard. It connects your Colombian bank account directly to the casino for near-instant deposits. Almost every Coljuegos-licensed operator accepts it.

Can I use Nequi at online casinos?

Yes, many licensed Colombian casinos accept Nequi, the Bancolombia-backed mobile wallet. Deposits are instant from your phone, and you do not share bank details with the casino.

How does Efecty work for casino deposits?

Efecty is a cash voucher system with thousands of payment points across Colombia. You pay cash at an Efecty point, receive a voucher code, and redeem it at the casino cashier. It is useful if you do not use online banking.

Do Colombian banks block gambling transactions?

Some Colombian banks block or flag gambling-related card transactions, especially at unlicensed offshore sites. PSE transfers to licensed operators generally work smoothly because the operator is a recognized Colombian business.

Can I deposit crypto at Colombian casinos?

Coljuegos-licensed operators generally do not accept crypto. International crypto casinos accept Bitcoin and USDT from Colombian players, but they are not licensed by Coljuegos, so you lose Colombian regulatory protection.

Is there tax on casino deposits in Colombia?

Yes. Since March 2026, licensed operators collect a 16% consumption tax on deposits to online gambling platforms at the moment of deposit. This reduces the playable balance from your deposit.
