What PSE Is

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Linea) is Colombia's national online bank-transfer system. It lets you pay online straight from your bank account without a credit card. When you choose PSE at a casino cashier, you pick your bank from a list, get redirected to your bank's own website, log in there, and approve the transfer. The money usually lands in your casino account within minutes.

PSE is the default payment method at Coljuegos-licensed operators - almost every licensed Colombian casino accepts it. For a deeper breakdown of how it works, fees, and limits, see our PSE payment method guide.

What to Look For in a PSE Casino

  • Coljuegos license first. Check the official Coljuegos list before anything else. A casino that accepts PSE but is not on the list is not licensed in Colombia.
  • PSE withdrawals, not just deposits. Many casinos accept PSE deposits but pay winnings only by bank transfer or another method. Check the cashier page for withdrawal options before depositing.
  • Deposit tax transparency. Since March 2026, licensed operators collect a 16% consumption tax on deposits at the moment of deposit. A good casino shows you exactly how much of your deposit becomes playable balance before you confirm.
  • KYC process. Licensed casinos must verify your identity. Expect to upload a cedula (Colombian ID) and proof of address. If a casino lets you deposit large amounts with zero verification, that is a red flag, not a perk.
  • Spanish-language support. A licensed Colombian casino should offer customer support in Spanish during Colombian hours.

How a PSE Deposit Works, Step by Step

  1. Log in to the casino and open the cashier or deposit page.
  2. Select PSE as the deposit method and enter the amount.
  3. Choose your bank from the list (Bancolombia, Davivienda, Banco de Bogota, etc.).
  4. You are redirected to your bank's secure site. Log in with your normal online banking credentials.
  5. Review the transfer details and approve.
  6. You return to the casino. The deposit is usually credited within minutes.

Never enter your bank password on the casino's own page. PSE always redirects you to your bank's website - if a site asks for banking credentials directly, close it immediately.

PSE Deposit Limits and Speed

Typical ranges at licensed casinos:

  • Minimum deposit: usually around 10,000 - 20,000 COP.
  • Maximum deposit: commonly up to 10,000,000 COP per transaction, though individual casinos set their own caps.
  • Speed: deposits are near-instant in most cases.
  • Fees: PSE itself typically charges no player fee. Watch for the 16% deposit tax collected by the operator since March 2026 - that is separate from any fee.

These are typical ranges, not guarantees. Always check the casino's cashier page for exact numbers.

FAQ

What is PSE and how does it work at casinos?

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Linea) is Colombia's bank-transfer payment system. At a casino cashier you select PSE, pick your bank, log in with your bank credentials on the bank's own secure page, and approve the transfer. Deposits are usually instant.

Is PSE safe for casino deposits?

PSE itself is secure: you authenticate on your own bank's website, so the casino never sees your banking password. The risk is at the casino side, so only deposit at Coljuegos-licensed operators from the official list.

Can I withdraw casino winnings to PSE?

Some licensed casinos support PSE withdrawals back to your bank account; others only accept PSE for deposits and pay winnings by bank transfer or another method. Check the casino's cashier page before you deposit.

Are there fees for PSE casino deposits?

PSE itself usually does not charge the player a fee, and most licensed casinos do not add one. However, since March 2026 Colombia applies a 16% consumption tax on deposits to online gambling platforms, which the operator collects at deposit time.

Which banks work with PSE?

PSE connects to Colombia's main banks, including Bancolombia, Banco de Bogota, Davivienda, Banco de Occidente, BBVA Colombia, and Banco AV Villas, among others. If your bank offers online banking in Colombia, it almost certainly works with PSE.
