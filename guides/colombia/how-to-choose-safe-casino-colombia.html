How to Check If an Online Casino Is Safe in Colombia (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

  1. Home
  2. Colombia
  3. Safety Checklist
TL;DR: Colombia makes verification easy: Coljuegos publishes an official list of licensed online operators. If the casino's exact domain is on that list, it is licensed. If not, do not deposit. A Coljuegos logo on the casino's own site proves nothing - only the official list counts.

Step 1: Check the Official Coljuegos List

This is the whole game in Colombia. Coljuegos maintains the official list of authorized online gambling operators on its website (coljuegos.gov.co).

  1. Go to coljuegos.gov.co.
  2. Find the section listing authorized online operators (juegos de suerte y azar operados por internet).
  3. Search for the exact domain of the casino you want to use.
  4. If the domain is listed, the operator holds a valid concession contract. If not, it is unlicensed in Colombia.

Do this before creating an account, not after depositing. It takes two minutes and is the single strongest protection available to a Colombian player.

Step 2: Ignore the Logo, Trust the List

Many unlicensed sites display a Coljuegos logo or claim "licensed in Colombia" in their footer. A logo is just an image - anyone can copy it. Scam sites do this routinely. The only verification that matters is the domain appearing on Coljuegos' own official list.

Step 3: Read the Terms in Spanish

A licensed Colombian operator must provide terms and conditions in Spanish. If the terms are only in English, or the Spanish version looks machine-translated and incomplete, treat that as a warning sign. Pay special attention to:

  • Withdrawal rules and timeframes.
  • Bonus wagering requirements (see our bonuses guide).
  • KYC and verification requirements.
  • Account closure and dormancy rules.

Step 4: Check Responsible-Gambling Tools

Coljuegos requires licensed operators to offer responsible-gambling measures. Before depositing, confirm the casino offers:

  • Deposit limits you can set yourself.
  • Session time reminders or limits.
  • Self-exclusion options.
  • Links to problem-gambling help resources.

If none of these exist, the operator is either unlicensed or non-compliant. Either way, do not play there.

Red Flags: Walk Away Immediately

  • Not on the Coljuegos list. The big one.
  • Pressure to deposit via crypto, gift cards, or P2P transfers. Licensed Colombian casinos use PSE, Nequi, cards, and bank transfers - not anonymous payment rails.
  • No KYC. Licensed operators must verify your identity (cedula). A casino that lets you deposit and withdraw large amounts with zero verification is not compliant.
  • Bonus terms you cannot find. If wagering requirements are hidden or the support agent cannot explain them, leave.
  • Blocked in Colombia but accessible via VPN pitch. If a site tells you to use a VPN to access it from Colombia, it is unlicensed. Coljuegos blocks such domains for a reason.

If Something Goes Wrong

At a licensed casino: complain to the operator in writing first (email or live chat transcript), keep records, and if unresolved, escalate to Coljuegos. Our complaints guide walks through the process with real case patterns.

At an unlicensed casino: you have no Colombian regulator to turn to. This is the real cost of playing offshore, and the main reason to stick to the official list.

FAQ

How do I verify a casino has a Coljuegos license?

Go to the Coljuegos website (coljuegos.gov.co) and find the official list of authorized online operators. Check that the exact domain you plan to use appears on the list. This is the only verification that counts.

What are red flags of an unsafe casino in Colombia?

No entry on the Coljuegos official list, no Spanish-language terms and conditions, no visible responsible-gambling tools, pressure to deposit via crypto or gift cards, and bonus terms you cannot find or read.

Can I trust a casino that shows a Coljuegos logo?

A logo on the casino's own site proves nothing - anyone can copy an image. Only the official Coljuegos list matters. Always cross-check the domain there.

What should I do before my first deposit?

Verify the Coljuegos license on the official list, complete KYC identity verification, read the bonus terms including wagering requirements, and set deposit limits in your account before playing.

Where can I complain about a licensed casino?

First complain to the casino in writing and keep records. If unresolved, you can escalate to Coljuegos as the regulator. See our complaints guide for a step-by-step process.
