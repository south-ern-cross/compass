Why SPEI Matters for Mexican Players

SPEI (Sistema de Pagos Electronicos Interbancarios) is Mexico's interbank payment system, operated by Banco de Mexico. It moves pesos between any two Mexican bank accounts in seconds, around the clock. No cards, no apps to install, no third party - just your regular banking app and a CLABE account number.

For casino players this is the closest thing to a local payment method: fast, free in most cases, and denominated in pesos. A casino that supports SPEI is a casino that has invested in serving the Mexican market. See our full SPEI payment guide for the mechanics.

FAQ

What is SPEI?

SPEI (Sistema de Pagos Electronicos Interbancarios) is Mexico's interbank electronic payment system, run by Banco de Mexico. It lets you send money between any Mexican bank accounts in seconds, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

How do I deposit at a casino with SPEI?

You open your banking app, choose a SPEI transfer, and send pesos to the CLABE account number the casino gives you. The money usually arrives within seconds to a few minutes. You need a Mexican bank account to use it.

Can I withdraw winnings with SPEI?

Most casinos that take SPEI deposits also pay withdrawals by SPEI, usually within minutes to a few hours once the withdrawal is approved. Check the casino's withdrawal times on its payments page before you deposit.

Is SPEI safe for casino payments?

SPEI itself is a bank-grade system operated by Banco de Mexico, and transfers are traceable. The risk is not the payment method but the casino: only send money to operators you have verified with our safety checklist.

Are there fees for SPEI casino deposits?

Most Mexican banks do not charge for SPEI transfers, and most casinos do not charge for deposits either. Some casinos may charge for withdrawals - check the terms on the operator's site before depositing.
