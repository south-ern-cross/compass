Casino Payment Methods in Nigeria (2026)

By Spinora Wins Editorial Team | Last updated: September 2026

  1. Home

TL;DR: Nigerian players have plenty of options: OPay and PalmPay for instant mobile money deposits, USSD for no-internet deposits, bank transfer and Verve cards for traditional routes, and crypto (BTC/USDT) for the fastest withdrawals. Crypto and e-wallets are usually free; bank-based methods can carry small fees.

Mobile Money: OPay and PalmPay

OPay and PalmPay are mobile money operators that have become the go-to deposit rails for Nigerian bettors. At casinos that support them, deposits are instant and withdrawals are typically processed within 24 hours, often much faster.

  • How it works: you fund your OPay or PalmPay wallet, then select it in the casino cashier and approve the transfer in the app.
  • Best for: everyday players who already use these apps for daily payments.
  • Watch out for: not every casino supports them yet. Check the cashier before you register. You will also need a funded wallet and the app installed.

Paga is another Nigerian mobile money service in the same category, and Kuda and Moniepoint (digital banks) are also used for betting transactions.

USSD: Deposits Without Internet

USSD codes let you deposit from any phone by dialing a short code. Your bank sends a confirmation prompt, you enter your PIN, and the money moves in seconds.

  • Best for: players with poor data connection or basic phones.
  • Limitation: deposit-only. You cannot withdraw via USSD, so you will need another method for payouts.
  • Fees: small bank charges usually apply per transaction.

Bank Transfer and Cards

Direct bank transfers work at most casinos serving Nigeria and suit larger deposits. Withdrawals via bank transfer typically take 1-3 business days.

For cards: Verve is Nigeria's domestic card scheme and is widely accepted at locally-oriented sites, usually processed through gateways like Paystack or Flutterwave. Visa and Mastercard also work where supported. International crypto casinos generally do not accept Verve.

Bank-based methods (transfer, USSD, cards) usually carry small fees per transaction. The exact amount depends on your bank and the method.

Crypto: BTC and USDT

Crypto is the fastest rail overall: deposits are instant after network confirmation, and withdrawals often land within minutes to a few hours. You do not need a naira account at all.

  • USDT is popular because it tracks the US dollar, so your balance does not swing with the market.
  • BTC is the most widely accepted coin at crypto casinos.
  • Security: always send to the exact address the casino shows you, and withdraw only to a wallet you control. Crypto transactions cannot be reversed.

FAQ

What is the fastest way to deposit at a casino from Nigeria?

Mobile money wallets like OPay and PalmPay offer instant deposits at casinos that support them. Crypto deposits (BTC, USDT) are also instant once the network confirms. USSD and bank transfers take a few minutes.

Can I use OPay or PalmPay at online casinos?

Yes, at casinos that support Nigerian payment rails. OPay and PalmPay are mobile money operators widely used for betting deposits in Nigeria. Availability varies by casino, so check the cashier page before registering.

Can I deposit with a Verve card?

Verve is Nigeria's domestic card scheme and is accepted at many locally-oriented betting sites, usually processed through gateways like Paystack or Flutterwave. International crypto casinos typically do not accept Verve.

Are there fees for casino deposits in Nigeria?

Bank-based methods (USSD, bank transfer, debit cards) often carry small fees per transaction depending on the method. E-wallet and crypto deposits are often free, but always check the casino's cashier for the exact terms.

How do I withdraw winnings to Nigeria?

Crypto withdrawals to your own wallet are usually fastest (minutes to hours). E-wallet withdrawals via OPay take up to 24 hours. Bank transfers take 1-3 business days. Most casinos require identity verification before your first withdrawal.

What is USSD deposit and how does it work?

USSD lets you deposit using a short code dialed on any phone, no internet needed. Your bank sends a prompt, you confirm with your PIN, and the money moves. It is deposit-only: you cannot withdraw via USSD.
