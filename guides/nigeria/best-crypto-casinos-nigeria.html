How to Deposit Crypto From Nigeria

  1. Get a crypto wallet. Any reputable wallet app works. Keep your recovery phrase private and offline.
  2. Buy BTC or USDT. Use a Nigerian crypto exchange or a P2P market. USDT is popular because it tracks the US dollar, so your balance stays stable.
  3. Register at the casino. Use a real email. Most crypto casinos ask for minimal personal details at signup.
  4. Send crypto to the deposit address. The casino shows you a wallet address. Triple-check it before sending - crypto transactions cannot be reversed.
  5. Play. Your balance appears after the network confirms the transaction, usually within minutes.

Withdrawing Your Winnings

Request a withdrawal to your own wallet address. The casino processes it, and the coins arrive after network confirmation. First withdrawals sometimes trigger a KYC check (ID and proof of address) - this is normal and required by the casino's license. Complete it once and later withdrawals are faster.

Important: always withdraw to a wallet you control, never to an exchange deposit address you do not recognize. And never share your private keys with anyone, including casino support.
