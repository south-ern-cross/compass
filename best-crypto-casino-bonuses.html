What "wagering" actually means (with real math)

BitStarz explains it plainly on its official blog: a 30x wagering requirement means you bet the bonus money 30 times. Deposit $100, get $100 bonus, and you must place $100 x 30 = $3,000 in bets before the bonus and its winnings can be withdrawn.

Now the math most bonus lists skip. At a typical slot RTP of 96%, every $100 you bet costs you about $4 on average. Clearing that 40x requirement on a $100 bonus means $4,000 in bets - about $160 in expected losses. That is more than the $100 bonus itself. This is why the headline is not the value: the wagering is.

Two more traps from the fine print:

  • Game contribution. Table games carry lower house edges, so casinos exclude them or limit their contribution to clearing (Cashy says this outright in its official editorial). If you play blackjack to clear a slots bonus, you may clear almost nothing.
  • Bonus staging. Big packages are split across deposits for a reason: BitStarz pays its 180 free spins 20 a day over 8 days, and Cloudbet's rewards calendar forfeits anything you do not claim within 24 hours. Miss a day, lose part of the bonus.

Run the numbers yourself before claiming anything: Wagering Calculator and Bonus EV Calculator.

Our benchmark: 30x and below is tolerable, 50x+ is nearly unplayable

This is our editorial rule of thumb, not an industry standard:

  • 0x (zero wagering): the bonus is worth what it says. Rare. Cashy runs every bonus this way.
  • Up to 30x: tolerable if the time limit is reasonable and the max bet is not tiny. Still usually negative expected value at normal RTP, but clearable.
  • 40x: the industry average. Clearable with luck, but expect the house edge to eat most of it.
  • 50x and above: nearly unplayable. At 50x on a $100 bonus you must bet $5,000 - about $200 in expected losses at 96% RTP. Twice the bonus, gone before you start.

The number alone is not enough: a 30x bonus with a 7-day deadline and a $2 max bet can be harder to clear than a 40x bonus with 30 days. Compare all the columns, not just one.

FAQ

What is wagering in a casino bonus?

Wagering (playthrough) is how many times you must bet the bonus before withdrawing it. A 30x wagering on a $100 bonus means $3,000 in bets first. BitStarz uses exactly this example on its official blog.

What wagering is reasonable for a crypto casino bonus?

Our rule of thumb: 30x and below is tolerable, 40x is average, 50x or more is nearly unplayable. At 96% slot RTP, clearing 40x on a $100 bonus costs about $160 in expected losses - more than the bonus. Zero-wagering bonuses are worth exactly what they say.

Which crypto casino has the best welcome bonus?

Can I withdraw bonus winnings without limits?
