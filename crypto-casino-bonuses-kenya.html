Bonus types you can claim in Kenya

  • Welcome deposit match - The standard offer: the casino matches your first deposit by a percentage. Crypto deposits usually qualify, but always check the terms.
  • Free spins - Often bundled with the welcome package or offered as standalone promos on popular slots.
  • No-deposit bonuses - Real but rare: a small cash amount or spins just for registering. Expect high wagering and low cashout caps.
  • Cashback and rakeback - A percentage of losses or turnover returned, often with low or no wagering attached.
  • Reloads and VIP rewards - Smaller matches on later deposits plus loyalty perks for regular players.

What to check before claiming

  • Wagering multiplier - How many times you must bet the bonus before withdrawing. Lower is better; anything above 40-50x is harsh.
  • Max bet while wagering - Exceeding it can void the bonus and the winnings.
  • Game weighting - Slots usually count 100%; table and live games count much less or zero.
  • Minimum deposit, expiry, cashout cap - The three numbers that decide the real value of the offer.
  • Geo-restrictions - Some promotions exclude specific countries. Check the bonus terms for your country, not just the homepage banner.
