Casino Terms Glossary

Last updated: September 2026 by the Spinora Wins editorial team.

RTP (Return to Player)
The percentage of wagered money a game pays back over time. A 96% RTP slot returns $96 per $100 wagered on average. See our RTP database.
Wagering Requirement
How many times you must bet a bonus before withdrawing it. 35x on $100 means $3,500 in bets. Use our calculator.
Volatility
How risky a slot is. Low volatility pays small wins often. High volatility pays rarely but big.
House Edge
The casino's mathematical advantage as a percentage. Lower is better for the player.
Max Win
The most a slot can pay on one spin, shown as a multiple of bet (e.g. 5,000x).
Paylines
Lines on which matching symbols pay.
Provably Fair
Crypto-casino system letting you verify each bet was random via cryptographic hashes.
KYC
Identity verification: ID plus proof of address, required before large withdrawals.
Free Spins
Bonus spins. Winnings may carry wagering unless stated "no wagering".
Rakeback
Refund of part of the house edge. Common in crypto casinos.
Cashback
Refund of part of your losses, usually weekly.
