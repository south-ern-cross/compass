Status: Fully regulated market. Colombia was the first country in Latin America to legalize and regulate online gambling (Law 1753 of 2015, licensing since 2016), overseen by the state regulator Coljuegos. Only Coljuegos-licensed operators may legally offer games to Colombian residents. Recent changes: a 19% VAT on player deposits was introduced in 2025, followed by Decree 0240 (12 March 2026) establishing a 16% national consumption tax on online gambling, applied at the moment of deposit; in October 2025 Coljuegos published a draft resolution capping bonuses converted into gaming credits; a Special Regulatory Unit was created the same month; and in June 2026 the Council of State suspended key advertising rules for online operators pending a final decision. None of this changes the core rule: the fully legal option for Colombian players is a Coljuegos-licensed site.
TL;DR: Colombia runs a mature, state-monopoly regulated market: operators need a Coljuegos concession contract, pay exploitation rights plus taxes (a significant share funds public healthcare), and PSE is the standard deposit rail. How It Works

Law 1753 of 2015 gave Colombia's state gambling monopoly an online dimension, and Coljuegos began issuing online concession contracts in 2016 - making Colombia the first regulated online gambling market in Latin America. Licensed operators pay fixed and variable exploitation rights plus taxes; gambling revenue is a major funding stream for the public health system, with Coljuegos reporting more than COP 4 trillion (roughly USD 970 million) transferred to healthcare since 2022.

The market keeps evolving. In 2025 a temporary 19% VAT was applied to player deposits, and operators began compensating players with bonuses - prompting Coljuegos to draft bonus-to-credit caps (published for public comment October-November 2025). Decree 0240, signed 12 March 2026, replaced the VAT approach with a 16% national consumption tax on online gambling, levied on gross gaming revenue and collected by operators at deposit time - including crypto deposits. In October 2025 Coljuegos also created a Special Regulatory Unit to modernize oversight, announced a National Gaming Regulation Plan for 2026, and legalized keno (rollout from January 2026). Meanwhile, in June 2026 the Council of State suspended some of Coljuegos' advertising restrictions after operators challenged them - a final ruling is still pending.

For players, the practical takeaway is simple: a site with a Coljuegos concession is the only fully legal way to gamble online from Colombia. Offshore casinos - including the crypto-first brands on our lists - are not Coljuegos-licensed. They accept Colombian players under their own terms of service (where not explicitly restricted), but they operate outside Colombian consumer protections, and Colombian tax rules on deposits still apply to the operator side.

Payment Methods

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Línea) is the standard deposit rail at Coljuegos-licensed sites - a bank-to-merchant transfer gateway covering virtually all Colombian banks, with near-instant settlement and minimum deposits typically around 20,000-50,000 COP. Nequi and Efecty cover mobile-first and cash-preferring users; debit and credit cards are also widely supported. At offshore crypto casinos, players typically deposit in BTC, ETH, USDT and other cryptocurrencies instead.

FAQ

Is online gambling legal in Colombia?

Yes - for adults 18+. Colombia has run a fully regulated online market since 2016 under Coljuegos. Only operators holding a Coljuegos concession contract are fully legal for Colombian residents.

What is Coljuegos?

Coljuegos is Colombia's state-owned gambling regulator, in charge of the state monopoly on games of chance. It licenses online operators, collects exploitation rights and taxes, and channels a large share of revenue into public healthcare.

What taxes apply to online gambling in 2026?

Under Decree 0240 (signed 12 March 2026), a 16% national consumption tax applies to online gambling, calculated on gross gaming revenue and collected by operators at deposit time. This followed a temporary 19% VAT on deposits introduced in 2025.

What is PSE?

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Línea) is Colombia's online banking payment gateway for secure bank-to-merchant transfers - the standard deposit method at Coljuegos-licensed casinos. Deposits post near-instantly and usually start around 20,000-50,000 COP.
