FAQ

What is a crypto casino?

A casino that accepts cryptocurrency deposits and pays withdrawals back in crypto. Most accept Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDT. Deposits credit fast and withdrawals are usually much quicker than bank payouts.

It changes often, so compare current offers in our Bonus Comparator. Right now the strongest packages are BitStarz (100% up to 1 BTC + 180 free spins), Cloudbet (up to $2,500 with zero wagering) and Vave (150% up to 1.5 BTC). Always check wagering terms before you pick.

Are the casinos listed here safe?

Every listed casino is checked against our published rating method: licensing, payout track record, bonus terms, support quality and reputation. Still, start with a small test deposit and one withdrawal before playing bigger - that rule applies everywhere. Casinos we cannot verify end up on our blacklist.

What is a wagering requirement?

How many times you must bet a bonus amount before withdrawing it. A 35x requirement on 100 USDT means 3,500 USDT in bets. Zero-wagering offers (like Cloudbet and Duelbits free spins) let you keep what you win. Use our Wagering Calculator to see what any bonus really costs.

Can I play with USDT?

Yes, most casinos in our top 10 accept USDT (Tether) alongside BTC and ETH. USDT is popular because its value does not swing like Bitcoin. See our guide to USDT casinos for details.

Do crypto casinos give free spins?

Yes. BitStarz gives 180 free spins with its welcome package, Duelbits offers up to 500 free spins, and several others include spins in their bonuses. Compare them on our free spins page.

What is the minimum deposit?

Usually $10 to $20 in crypto equivalent. BitStarz and most others start at $20. Cloudbet has no fixed minimum on some methods. The exact amount is always shown on the casino cashier page before you send funds.

How fast are crypto withdrawals?

Typically minutes to a few hours after the casino approves the request - far faster than bank wires or card payouts, which can take days. First withdrawals can take longer because of identity checks.

Do I need to verify my identity?

It depends on the casino. Some let you play with just an email; others ask for ID before the first withdrawal or above a threshold. Assume a big win can trigger a verification check anywhere.
