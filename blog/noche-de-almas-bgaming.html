Noche de Almas (BGaming): Dia de los Muertos Slot with Cluster Pays and the Reel Deal Feature

Published 15 October 2026. BGaming closes its Halloween seasonal set on 20 October with Noche de Almas, a high-volatility slot inspired by the Mexican Dia de los Muertos: Cluster Pays, Coin Respins and the studio’s first Reel Deal feature, which compresses 500 spins into a reveal of the three best results.

A dark take on Dia de los Muertos

Most Day of the Dead slots reach for bright marigolds and neon sugar skulls. Noche de Almas goes the other way: BGaming built it around a dark, high-contrast look that stands apart from the usual cheerful takes on the holiday. The story follows two souls on their way to a reunion, with love crossing the boundary between life and death as the emotional hook.

Cluster Pays and Coin Respins

The base game runs on Cluster Pays instead of paylines, so wins form when matching symbols land in connected groups. Coin Respins add the second layer: coin symbols on the reels can trigger a respin-style sequence where coins stick in place and every new coin keeps the round alive.

Reel Deal: 500 spins in one reveal

The headline act is Reel Deal, a first for BGaming’s portfolio. Players can buy 500 spins at a lower cost per spin than playing them one by one. The spins run in the background and, instead of watching each one, the player gets a reveal of the three best results, shown from the lowest to the highest. It is a shortcut for players who want volume without the wait.

The last stop of BGaming’s Halloween set

Noche de Almas is the final release in BGaming’s Halloween seasonal set, following Spider Circus Hold and Win (19 October), Aviamasters 2 Halloween, Chicken Halloween and Witchcraft Riches. Julia Alekseeva, the studio’s CPO, said the set lets players experience the spooky season in their own way, and called Noche de Almas a dark but hopeful Dia de los Muertos adventure.
