What is new in Big Bass Splash Halloween 1000

The game is a seasonal reskin rather than a new engine: the Money symbol collection mechanic stays intact while the lake gets a Halloween makeover - pumpkins, bats, mummified fish and a fisherman dressed as Count Dracula. The genuine mechanical shift is the risk profile: volatility is eased to medium against the high-variance base game.

In free spins, Fisherman Wilds collect Money symbols worth up to 1,000x the bet, and every fourth Fisherman collected retriggers the feature with a collection multiplier that climbs to 10x. A new pumpkin symbol can land during free spins to award mid-bonus upgrades, layered on top of the ante bet and the 100x / 450x bonus buy options familiar across the range.
