What is new in Trials of Zeus

The headline change is the Wonder engine. Instead of treating reveals as an occasional modifier, Trials of Zeus builds almost the entire game around them: Wonder symbols reveal paying symbols, Wilds or the more valuable Wonder Reels, and every revealed symbol that contributes to a win turns sticky and triggers a respin. The sequence keeps going until no new win lands or a Wonder Reel appears.

Three bonus games - the Triple Triumph - push the system further with sticky Wonder symbols throughout, a Wonder meter that fills as Wonders land, and the potential to fill the entire grid. Wonder Reels steer the big payouts toward cash prizes and multipliers.

The launch is exclusive: you will find the game at selected casinos only, not everywhere at once. The math sits on a 6x5 grid with 19 fixed paylines, a default RTP of 96.26% and a maximum payout of 20,000x the bet, in line with the high-volatility profile Hacksaw is known for.
