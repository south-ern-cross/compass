How the Hold & Win bonus works

Landing 6 or more Spider symbols anywhere on the reels opens the Hold & Win bonus. The spiders stick in place while the remaining positions respin, and each new spider resets the spin counter and locks in too. Multipliers and prize values land on the spiders as the round goes on, and Progressive Mystery symbols can grow behind the scenes before revealing their values. Fill the whole grid and the full 5,000x maximum win is yours.

Expanding Wilds and Line Choice

Outside the bonus, Expanding Wilds can stretch to cover full reels and pull scattered wins together. The Line Choice feature lets players pick their payline setup before spinning, a small touch that changes how the spider symbols land across the grid. BGaming built the game on its proven classic mechanics, pairing the spider collection loop with a circus setting the studio calls one of the fastest-growing themes in its portfolio.
