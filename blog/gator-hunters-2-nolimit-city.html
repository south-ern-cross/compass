Gator Hunters 2 (Nolimit City): Fire Zone, 3,000x Golden Bullet and 30,000x Max Win

Published 9 October 2026. Nolimit City sent its swamp crew back out on 6 October with Gator Hunters 2, the sequel to last year's Gator Hunters. The 6x5 scatter-pays grid is back, but this time a Fire Zone drops onto the reels on every spin and upgrades whatever special symbols land inside it. The headline number is 30,000x the bet - one of the biggest max-win caps Nolimit has ever shipped.

How it plays

Wins land when 8 or more matching symbols appear anywhere on the 6x5 grid, and winning symbols vanish so new ones can drop in. The Fire Zone is the twist: at the start of each spin it covers a random 2x2, 3x3, 4x4 or 5x6 area, and any special symbol that lands inside gets upgraded. Wilds pick up a random x2, x3 or x10 multiplier, Eaters become Super Eaters, Bonus symbols become Super Bonus symbols, and Revolvers get higher-value bullets.

Eaters do the cleanup job: when no wins remain, a Normal Eater removes a random batch of symbols and turns into a Wild, while a Super Eater does the same but adds a multiplier to the Wild. Then the Revolvers spin. Each one holds bullets and empty chambers - a selected bullet adds its multiplier to the total win multiplier. There are 11 bullet types, from a 2x purple bullet to the 3,000x Golden Bullet, and up to six Revolvers can sit on the grid at once.

Three Bonus symbols trigger 7 Gatornado Spins; four trigger 9 Fire Gatornado Spins, where the Fire Zone never drops below 4x4 and every Revolver is a Super Fire Revolver that can force the others to spin again. To skip the queue, players can buy 7 Bullet Juicer spins for 390x the bet, or pick from four Nolimit Boosters: Bonus Booster (3x), Kill Spin (8x), Overkill Spin (50x) and Massacre Spin (1,400x). Side Hustle lets you wager on outcomes like a character getting removed or the total multiplier crossing 100x.
