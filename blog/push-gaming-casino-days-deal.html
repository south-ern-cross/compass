Push Gaming Supplies Slots to New Casino Days Brand

Published 3 October 2026. Push Gaming has signed a content supply deal with Rhino Entertainment: its slots, including Razor Shark, The Shadow Order and the newly launched Mystery Museum, are now live at Casino Days, the operator's new brand.

What the deal covers

Under the deal, Push Gaming content is now available at Casino Days. The launch titles named are Razor Shark and The Shadow Order, plus the recently launched Mystery Museum - a 5x3, 10-payline slot built around a museum tour where each artifact has its own story.

Casino Days has also recently gone live with content from Playson and Relax Gaming, among other major providers, as the new brand builds out its lobby.

Why it matters

Push Gaming's portfolio now holds more than 20 online slots, and the supplier has been scaling fast. It acquired online gambling technology provider Game Server Integrations (GSI), which let it deliver content directly to operator partners, and in late January it secured licences from the UK Gambling Commission and the Malta Gaming Authority.

The deal moves Push Gaming from boutique game developer toward full-service supplier - and gives Casino Days a recognisable set of titles for launch.

What the executives said

Push Gaming CEO James Marshall said the partnership "further enhanced" the company's market position and gives it room to "continue the strategic expansion which has always been a focal point for the team".

Rhino Entertainment managing director Ross Parkhill said "having all the best and most immersive gaming content is key for us", and that adding Push Gaming's slots "has already proved a great move".
