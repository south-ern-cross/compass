Cloudforge (Relax Gaming): Release Date, RTP and Max Win

Published 3 October 2026. A goblin pilot hunts a swarm above the clouds: Relax Gaming's steampunk slot is built around expanding wilds with growing multipliers, electric reels and two free spins modes with a global multiplier.

What is new in Cloudforge

Wilds land with multipliers and expand upwards, adding 1x for each step of the expansion. If two wilds land on the same reel, the higher one expands first; when they coincide, their multipliers are multiplied together. The Electric Reels feature can randomly electrify reels, so wilds expand both up and down to fill the reel and double the multiplier.

Extermination Spins award 8 free spins where every wild that lands feeds a global multiplier applied to all wins. Eradication Spins work the same way but guarantee one electric reel on every spin. Hit frequency is quoted at 34.93%. The math is medium-high volatility with a 96.1% headline RTP and a 10,000x maximum.
