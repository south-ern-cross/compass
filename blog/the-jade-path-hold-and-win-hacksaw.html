The Jade Path Hold & Win (Hacksaw): Queen Aisha guards a 2,500x jungle jackpot

Published 18 October 2026. Hacksaw Gaming released The Jade Path Hold & Win on 15 October, a Mesoamerican jungle slot built around the studio’s take on the classic Hold & Win. The 5x3 grid with 9 paylines pairs Coin Orbs and Jaguar Multipliers with a Bonus Choice menu: sticky-wild free spins or a pre-loaded Hold & Win round. RTP: 96.19%, medium volatility.

A slower kind of Hacksaw

Hacksaw Gaming made its name on brutal volatility and five-figure multipliers, so The Jade Path Hold & Win lands as something of a change of pace. This is a medium-volatility slot with a 2,500x ceiling, aimed at players who want the studio’s polish without the punishment. The setting is a Mesoamerican jungle of headdresses, masks and jaguars, watched over by Queen Aisha, a jade-eyed warrior who stands beside the grid. Despite the name, don’t expect an Asian theme: the jade here belongs to Aztec-style treasure lore.

Orbs, jackpots and the jaguar

Any combination of three Coin Orb or Jackpot Orb symbols triggers the Jade Hold & Win with three refilling lives. Coin Orbs carry values from 0.2x to 50x the bet, while five fixed Jackpot Orbs sit above them: Mini at 5x, Minor at 25x, Major at 250x, Mega at 500x and Grand at 2,500x. Jaguar Multipliers landing during the round apply x2 to x20 to every Coin Orb in view, though they leave the jackpots alone. Fill the whole grid and every orb value pays out at once before the board clears and the round continues.

Pick your bonus

Three bonus symbols open the Bonus Choice menu, and the two options play nothing alike. Warrior Riches awards 10 free spins where the Warrior Wild stays sticky for the whole round, and any Jaguar Multiplier acts as a wild multiplier added to the sticky wilds on a winning spin. Jade Bounty Hold & Win runs the Hold & Win rules on a grid that starts pre-loaded with at least five special symbols, and every Coin Orb in it is worth at least 1x. Two buys sit in the menu: BonusHunt FeatureSpins at 2x the bet, which triple the bonus odds per spin, and a direct Jade Bonus Choice at 100x.

The numbers

The Jade Path Hold & Win runs at 96.19% RTP in its default configuration, with operator versions ranging up to 96.3% and down to 86.28%, so the paytable is worth a glance before playing. Volatility is medium at 3 out of 5, the maximum win is 2,500x the bet, and stakes run from 0.10 to 400 EUR per spin.
