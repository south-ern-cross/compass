Aviamasters 2 Halloween (BGaming): Pumpkinhead Crash Game with 97% RTP

Published 13 October 2026. BGaming launches Aviamasters 2 Halloween on 15 October: the crash-style #Casual hit gets a spooky reskin. The pilot is a pumpkinhead, ghosts replace the rockets and the 97.00% RTP stays exactly where it was.

How a round works

Aviamasters 2 Halloween is not a slot: you set a stake and the plane takes off on a random flight path. Multipliers appear along the route and add to the round balance as the plane collects them, while ghosts drifting across the path halve the balance when they catch it. The round pays only if the plane lands on an island; a splash into the sea loses the stake.

The Halloween reskin keeps the proven math of Aviamasters 2 and changes the cast: the pilot is a pumpkinhead, the rockets that used to halve the balance are ghosts, the Nitro booster is now a Nitro Potion, and the tropical backdrop is gone, replaced by stormy seas and thick fog.

Boosters and Safe Landing

Four boosters are scattered across the haunted skies: Magnet, Laser Gun, Nitro Potion and Life Buoy, each bending the flight in its own way. Safe Landing can be switched on from the very first round for 50x the bet: the plane is guaranteed a winning landing every round, even when the island is still far away.

With medium-low volatility and a hit rate BGaming lists at 2.5, the game is built for frequent small landings rather than rare big ones, and the 1,000x ceiling caps every flight.

Part of BGaming's Halloween set

Aviamasters 2 Halloween is the third release in BGaming's five-game Halloween set, following Witchcraft Riches and Chicken Halloween, and ahead of Spider Circus Hold and Win on 19 October and Noche de Almas on 20 October.

“We have received excellent feedback from our previous seasonal sets from both operators and players. Our latest Halloween offering allows players to experience the spooky season in their own way, with the five titles covering each of our three game worlds,” said Julia Alekseeva, chief product officer at BGaming.
