Forever Split Megaways (Pragmatic Play): Release Date, RTP and Max Win

Published 2 October 2026. An undead couple splits up after centuries together: Pragmatic Play's Forever Split Megaways turns a vampire divorce into a 6-reel Megaways slot with up to 72,030 ways to win, a Settlement Multiplier fed by special horizontal reels, and free spins where that multiplier never resets.

What is new in Forever Split Megaways

The Settlement Multiplier is the mechanic that defines this release. Paying symbols landing on the horizontal reels above and below the main grid can carry random multipliers from 1x to 15x. When such a symbol is part of a win, its value feeds a running Settlement Multiplier that applies to every later win in the same tumble sequence.

Free spins take the idea further: the Settlement Multiplier becomes persistent for the whole bonus instead of resetting, and every paying symbol on the horizontal reels is guaranteed to carry a multiplier. Three scatters award 10 free spins, with 5 extra for each additional scatter, up to 25. The math is medium volatility with a headline 96.58% RTP and a 10,000x ceiling - modest by Megaways standards, but the persistent multiplier gives the bonus round real momentum.
