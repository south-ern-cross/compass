How it plays

Winning symbols disappear through cascading refills, letting replacements drop in and potentially chain another win. Multiplier Potions can land during these sequences carrying values from x2 up to x500; once no further win is possible, all visible multiplier values are added together and applied to the total win of the sequence.

Four, five or six Sorceress Scatters award 15, 16 or 17 free spins plus scatter pays of 3x, 5x or 100x the bet. During the bonus, multipliers from winning spins feed a persistent Total Multiplier that keeps accumulating through the round, and landing three or more extra scatters adds five more free spins.
