What is new in Dr Zappo

The headline mechanic is the EyeBall Wild. EyeBall symbols land on the bottom row and shoot in one of three directions - up, diagonally left or diagonally right - turning every pay symbol in their path into a wild. Some wilds reveal additive multipliers from x2 to x200, and when several wild multipliers take part in the same win, their values are added together before the payout.

Three Energy Fields sit on the left, top and right edges of the grid. An EyeBall that hits a field bounces off and keeps travelling at the same angle; a head-on hit sends it straight back the way it came, adding multiplier values to the wilds it created along the path. Three bonus rounds - Eyeconic, No-Brainer and It's Electrifying - build on the same system, and a bonus buy is available for direct access.

The math is medium volatility with a default RTP of 96.31% and a maximum payout of 10,000x the bet. The game went into early access at crypto casinos in late August - Stake ran a tournament on it in the first half of September - before the general release on 29 September.
