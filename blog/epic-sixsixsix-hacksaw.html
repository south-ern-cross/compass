Epic SixSixSix (Hacksaw Gaming): the devil gets an Epic makeover with a 20,666x ceiling

Published 20 October 2026. Hacksaw Gaming releases Epic SixSixSix on 22 October, an Epic rework of its 2024 devil cartoon slot with higher stats and a bigger feature-buy menu. 5x4 grid with 14 paylines, 96.27% RTP, high volatility, Blue and Red Wicked Wheels, three free spins tiers and a 20,666x maximum win.

The devil gets an Epic makeover

Epic SixSixSix takes the 2024 original's 1930s cartoon hell - Balthazar and Beelzebub on pitchfork duty, cracked concrete walls, satanic symbols - and adds teal flames and a fresh coat of colour. The stats move with the visuals: the maximum win climbs from 16,666x to 20,666x the bet, and the base RTP rises from 96.15% to 96.27%. The 5-reel, 4-row grid with 14 paylines stays, and there are still no wilds or scatters: everything runs through the wheels.

Everything runs through the Wicked Wheels

Blue or red "6" symbols can land on reels 1, 3 and 5 - one per reel - and each one summons a Wicked Wheel in its position. In the base game only Blue wheels appear: they can award the max win, adding multipliers from 5x to 100x, multiplying multipliers from x2 to x10, or a "6" symbol that triggers the bonuses. Red wheels are the aggressive version, with adding multipliers from 10x to 500x and multiplying multipliers from x3 to x20. When several wheels fire at once, their values are collected from left to right.

Three bonus tiers and a deal with the devil

The number of "6" results on wheels decides the bonus: one six opens Speak of the Devil (10 free spins with more wheels), two sixes open Let Hell Break Loose! (10 spins, red wheels favoured), and three sixes open What the Hell (10 spins with a Red Wicked Wheel guaranteed on every spin). Before the first two rounds start, the player can strike a Deal with the Devil: a separate wheel offers 5, 8, 12, 16 or 20 spins, or a "6" that upgrades the bonus to the next tier. The deal can help or hurt, but it never downgrades the round.

The buy menu grows up

The feature-buy list doubles down on the volatility. Wicked FeatureSpins cost 50x for better wheel odds, Red Wicked FeatureSpins cost 100x and guarantee a red wheel on every spin, and High Roller FeatureSpins cost 2,000x with a stated 1-in-11 chance of hitting the max win on a single spin. The three bonus rounds can also be bought outright for 100x, 250x and 1,000x. At those prices this is clearly a game for deep bankrolls, not casual sessions.
