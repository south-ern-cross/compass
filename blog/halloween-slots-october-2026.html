New releases

Le Vampire (Hacksaw Gaming, 1 October) opens the month with a vampire theme - full details in our release post.

Timeless Halloween classics

New releases get the headlines, but these titles come back every October: Happy Halloween (Play'n GO, 96.51% RTP), Big Bass Halloween 2 and 3, Book of Shadows (Nolimit City), Possessed (Nolimit City, 96.06% RTP, 50,000x max win) and Mental 2 (Nolimit City, released 2025).

Two styles of Halloween slots

Halloween slots split into two camps. Friendly Halloween titles keep volatility low to medium and the scares cartoonish. Full horror goes the other way: high to extreme volatility, darker art, bigger swings. Pick the math that fits your bankroll, not just the theme.
