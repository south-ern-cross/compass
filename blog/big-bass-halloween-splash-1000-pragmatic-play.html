Big Bass Halloween Splash 1000 (Pragmatic Play): Count Dracula Takes the Rod

Published 16 October 2026. Pragmatic Play launched Big Bass Halloween Splash 1000 on 12 October, the fourth Halloween-themed entry in the Big Bass franchise. The game reuses the Splash 1000 engine from December 2025, dresses the fisherman as Count Dracula, eases volatility to medium and adds a pumpkin-pick bonus where players choose modifiers before the free spins.

The fourth Halloween Bass

This is the fourth Halloween entry in the Big Bass series, a franchise that held three of the UK top ten slots by player visibility at mid-2026. The base is Big Bass Splash 1000, released in December 2025, played on a 5x3 grid with 10 paylines. The seasonal version softens the profile: volatility drops from high to medium, and the max win goes from 25,000x to 20,000x the bet. The headline RTP is 96.50%, with 95.50% and 94.50% versions available to operators.

Pick your pumpkins before the bonus

Landing 3, 4 or 5 scatter symbols awards 10, 15 or 20 free spins. When only two scatters land, there is a random chance more get added to trigger the feature. Before the bonus starts, players pick from six pumpkins to reveal up to five modifiers: more wilds, more money symbols, extra free spins, starting the round at the second multiplier level, or a better chance of random features firing.

Fisherman collects, retriggers climb

During free spins, fisherman wilds collect the value of fish money symbols on the same spin, with values up to 1,000x. Every fourth fisherman retriggers the feature with 10 more free spins and a higher multiplier: 2x on the first retrigger, 3x on the second, 10x on the third and final one. If a fisherman lands without fish, or fish without a fisherman, one of three random features can add more of either. Pumpkin symbols can also land on the fifth reel during the bonus and upgrade it for the rest of the round.

Ante bet and bonus buys

An ante bet raises the stake by 50% and puts more scatters on the reels. Where regulations allow, the free spins can be bought for 100x the bet, and a super free spins round with a better shot at the 1,000x fish costs 450x. Sharon McHugh, Pragmatic Play’s PR director, said the game puts a supercharged spooky spin on the fishing franchise, with the pumpkin selection giving players more say over how the bonus plays out.
