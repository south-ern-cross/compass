25 Coins x3000 (Wazdan): Hold the Jackpot Slot with 3,000x Grand Jackpot Opens Cointober

Published 5 October 2026. Wazdan opened its five-week Cointober campaign on 1 October with 25 Coins x3000: a Hold the Jackpot slot where filling all 25 positions awards the 3,000x Grand Jackpot.

Official game art: Wazdan.

Key facts

ProviderWazdan
Release date1 October 2026
Bonus mechanicHold the Jackpot: 3 respins that reset with each new symbol
Grand Jackpot3,000x - fill all 25 positions
FeaturesSticky to Infinity, Cash Infinity symbols (5x-10x), Cluster Collector, Mini/Minor/Major jackpots
CampaignCointober: 1 October - 3 November 2026, 30+ Coins titles; closes with 36 Coins x3000 on 3 November

How the jackpot round works

The bonus round awards three respins that reset every time a new symbol lands on the grid. Mystery and Jackpot Mystery symbols carry over from the base game to the end of the bonus through the Sticky to Infinity feature, and the new Cash Infinity symbols land sticky with values between 5x and 10x, paid out in the final reveal.

The revamped Cluster Collector gathers values from adjacent Cash and Cash Infinity symbols across up to five spins, with the combined total paid when the bonus ends. Mini, Minor and Major jackpots can also land during the round.

Cointober campaign

“The Coins series has come a long way, and each new release gives our team another opportunity to build on what has worked while introducing something new for players,” said Andrzej Hyla, chief commercial officer at Wazdan.

The five-week Cointober push consolidates more than 30 Coins titles under a single brand identity and closes with a second new entry, 36 Coins x3000, on 3 November.
