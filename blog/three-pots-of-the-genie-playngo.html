Three Pots of the Genie (Play'n GO): Lock'n Gold Pots Explained

Published 3 October 2026. Play'n GO launches Three Pots of the Genie: a 6-reel video slot with 4,096 payways where three coloured pots above the reels - blue, red and purple - drive the Lock'n Gold bonus round.

How Lock'n Gold works

Above the reels sit three pots: blue, red and purple. Landing one or more Gem Scatters in the base game may trigger the Lock'n Gold round with one, two or all three pot features active.

Landed Coins stay locked through the Re-Spins, and their values stay hidden until the feature ends - unless a pot effect reveals them. Other Gem Scatters landing during Lock'n Gold may unlock additional pot features alongside the ones already active.

The three pots

Blue Pot (Double): a Double Scatter during Lock'n Gold reveals and doubles selected Coin values.

Red Pot (Ignite): an Ignite Scatter reveals and increases one selected Coin's value - and that Coin keeps increasing on every subsequent Re-Spin until the feature ends.

Purple Pot: landing Bonus Scatters reveals Mini, Minor, Major or Grand symbols and fills spaces on the corresponding panel; completing a panel awards its instant bonus.
