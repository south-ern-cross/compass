3 Pots of Aladdin (Tom Horn Gaming): three pots, seven Hold & Win setups and a 15,985x ceiling

Published 19 October 2026. Tom Horn Gaming releases 3 Pots of Aladdin on 22 October, a high-volatility 3x3 slot that rebuilds the classic Hold & Win around three themed pots: Jackpots, Collectors and Multipliers up to x10. RTP: 95.07%, max exposure 15,985.5x the bet.

One grid, three pots

3 Pots of Aladdin keeps the familiar Hold & Win skeleton and hangs three separate modifier systems on it. Red, green and blue coins feed their matching pots above the reels: Jackpots, Collectors and Multipliers reaching x10. Each pot can fire on its own or combine with the others, which is how the game gets to seven distinct bonus configurations from one 3x3 grid. The colour coding does the explaining mid-spin, so you always know which features are live without opening the paytable.

The Super version is the rare one

Every bonus round starts with three respins on the dedicated 3x3 grid, with each new coin locking in place and resetting the counter. Fill all nine positions and the Grand Jackpot lands. The headline configuration is the Super Hold & Win, which runs Jackpots, Collectors and Multipliers at the same time. It only opens when exactly one red, one green and one blue coin land together, so expect to see the single-pot versions far more often. Two bonus buys sit in the menu: a 50x buy for a Hold & Win with up to three special coins, and a 120x Super Buy that goes straight to the Super Hold & Win with all three pots active.

The numbers

Tom Horn Gaming lists the game at 95.07% RTP with high volatility and a maximum exposure of 15,985.5x the bet. That RTP sits below the 96% line most players treat as standard, so the paytable is worth a look at your casino. The release goes live on 22 October 2026, first in Latvia and Portugal, with wider rollout to follow.
