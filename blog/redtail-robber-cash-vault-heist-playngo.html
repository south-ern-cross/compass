Redtail Robber Cash Vault Heist (Play'n GO): Fox Collect, Mystery Cashpots and 15,000x Max Win

Published 10 October 2026. Play'n GO launched Redtail Robber Cash Vault Heist on 8 October: a cyberpunk heist slot starring Robbert Redtail the Fox, where the Fox symbol on reel 5 sweeps up every landed Coin value and Mystery symbols crack open Cashpots worth up to x100.

How it plays

Coin symbols land on any reel carrying values of x0.2, x0.4, x0.6, x0.8, x1, x2 or x5, but they pay nothing on their own. The Fox symbol appears on reel 5, substitutes for regular symbols in payline wins and activates Fox Collect: every landed Coin value is collected and paid at once. The Fox can also carry a random x2, x3, x5 or x10 multiplier, which applies both to wins it joins and to the collected Coins. Mystery symbols can land anywhere and open to reveal a Cashpot prize of x10, x20, x50 or x100, paid at the end of the spin.

The Bonus Game is a hold-and-win style round. Each Coin has a chance to trigger it, and landing six Coins in a single spin guarantees entry. The feature starts on an empty 5x4 grid with only Coin and Mystery symbols and three lives; each spin costs one life, and every new Coin or Mystery resets the lives back to three. When no lives remain, the combined value of all landed symbols is paid. With GO Ultra enabled, a Lucky Coin joins the round and either absorbs adjacent symbols or doubles their values, with individual symbol values capped at x5,000 the bet.

Non-paying scatters appear only on reels 2, 3 and 4, and three of them trigger 10 free spins. The Fox is placed at a random position at the start of the feature and stays on the reels, moving to a new position between spins with its multiplier re-randomised each time. Another three scatters add 10 more free spins, up to a maximum of 100.
