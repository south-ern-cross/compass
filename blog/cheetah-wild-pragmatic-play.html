Cheetah Wild (Pragmatic Play): Safari Collect Slot, 27x Multiplier Wilds and 5,000x Max Win

Published 10 October 2026. Pragmatic Play released Cheetah Wild on 8 October: a low-volatility safari slot on a 5x4 grid with 1,024 ways, where wilds on the middle reels carry 2x or 3x multipliers and a golden paw Collect symbol on reel 1 sweeps up Money values worth up to 100x.

How it plays

Elephants, rhinos, zebras and gazelles lead the paytable on 1,024 ways. Wilds land on reels 2, 3 and 4 carrying 2x or 3x multipliers, and when more than one wild joins the same win their values multiply together, so a full chain can reach 27x before it is applied. Money symbols worth 0.25x to 100x land on reels 2 to 5, while the golden paw Collect symbol appears on reel 1 and, whenever it connects through an adjacent left-to-right way, awards the sum of all Money values, boosted by any multiplier wilds in the chain.

Three, four or five scatters award 8, 15 or 20 free spins, and landing two to five more scatters during the round adds another 5 to 20 spins. During the feature, Level Up symbols feed a meter: collecting 4, 7, 13 and 15 of them unlocks five progressive levels, each converting another animal symbol into Money symbols for the rest of the round. Reach level five and every high-paying animal lands as a Money symbol until the feature ends. Players in permitted markets can also buy straight into the round: 100x for standard free spins, 500x for Super Free Spins, plus an ante bet option.
