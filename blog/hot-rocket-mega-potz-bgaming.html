Hot Rocket Mega Potz (BGaming): four volcanoes, a free-spins gamble and a jackpot wheel under a 6,030x ceiling

Published 26 October 2026. BGaming's Hot Rocket Mega Potz lands on 26 October 2026, the next chapter in the Rocket Eruption series. A 6x4 jungle grid with 4,096 ways to win pairs classic fruit and bell symbols with Coin and Collect symbols, four volcano bonus levels and a jackpot wheel on the side. 94.15% RTP, medium-high volatility, 6,030x max win.

Four volcanoes above a 6x4 jungle grid

The base game keeps the Rocket Eruption look: a tropical jungle frame, classic fruit, horseshoe and bell symbols, and a prize wheel on the left showing five jackpot tiers. Wins pay left to right on consecutive reels across 4,096 ways, and Wilds stand in for regular symbols. Coin symbols carry instant prizes, and Collect symbols gather every coin in view, so a single Collect landing can turn an ordinary grid into a payout worth watching. Bonus symbols work differently from the rest: each one lights a beast head below its reel, and that progress stays saved.

A pre-game gamble before the free spins

Four volcanoes sit above the grid, one for each X-Potz Bonus level: 9, 12, 15 and 18 free spins, each with its own Collect value from x2 to x5. Before the round starts, the game offers a gamble: try to upgrade to more spins and a bigger Collect multiplier, at the risk of a downgrade or losing the round outright. The upgrade can be attempted more than once, unless the first offer already lands on 18 spins with x5 Collect symbols. BGaming says progress toward bonus events through the beast heads is preserved when free spins trigger by another route, so a near miss does not wipe the board.

A Collect ladder for long sessions

Players who stay on the same game get a second path to bigger Collect values. Every 50 spins, the Collect multiplier steps up: x2, then x3, then x4, then x5, so by the 200th spin a random multiplier from x2 to x5 is always attached to Collect symbols. An Ante Bet toggle on the side panel doubles the chance of a bonus, and the buy screen in BGaming's own captures offers two ways in: a Free Spins Bonus and a Super Free Spins Bonus, priced at 100x and 200x the bet in that capture. Prices and availability of buy options vary by market, so the in-game panel at a licensed casino is the figure that counts.

Jackpots on a wheel

Alongside the free spins, Jackpot tokens feed the five jackpot tiers, and the jackpot round can trigger at any time with a guaranteed reward. The wheel asks for three matching sections to pay out, with Mini, Minor, Major, Mega and Max tiers on the rim. BGaming lists a 241,200 euro max win for the game, which matches 6,030x a 40 euro stake, the top of the listed stake range. The 94.15% RTP is the single figure BGaming publishes for this title.
