Wild Cash Gold (BGaming): Wild Cash Series Returns with a 25,000x Bonus Game

Published 15 October 2026. BGaming brings back its Wild Cash series on 3 November with Wild Cash Gold: a 5x3 #Classic slot with fruit-filled reels, a three-stage Bonus game where multipliers can climb to the 25,000x maximum win, the series-first Chance x2 feature and several Buy Bonus options.

The Wild Cash series is back

Wild Cash Gold keeps the series identity intact: fruit-filled reels and a glossy retro look, now with a gold treatment. It is a 5x3 #Classic slot, the game world BGaming reserves for its straightforward, hit-driven titles.

A three-stage Bonus game up to 25,000x

The Bonus game plays out in three stages, with multipliers that can build toward the 25,000x maximum win. The staged structure means the round escalates instead of paying out flat: each stage raises the ceiling on what the multipliers can deliver.

Chance x2 and Buy Bonus options

Chance x2 is new to the Wild Cash series and doubles down on the bonus chase. On top of that, several Buy Bonus options give players a priority pass straight into the Bonus game instead of waiting for the trigger.

BGaming’s November roadmap

Wild Cash Gold lands on 3 November, right after Hot Rocket Mega Potz (26 October) and Khonsu Merge Up (2 November), with Formula x10000 following on 5 November. It is BGaming’s classic-leaning answer to a month otherwise packed with #Entertainment releases.
