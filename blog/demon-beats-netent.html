Demon Beats (NetEnt): Avalanche Slot with x1024 Multiplier Ladder and 10,000x Max Win

Published 13 October 2026. NetEnt launches Demon Beats on 15 October: a 5x4 Avalanche slot set in a rain-slick Tokyo back alley where even losing spins can clear the board, the multiplier ladder climbs to x1024 and Super Free Spins never reset it.

How the multiplier ladder works

Every win starts an Avalanche: winning symbols vanish, new ones drop in and wins are evaluated again until the spin stops paying. Under the reels sits the multiplier ladder. It starts at x1 and climbs a step with every win or colossal explosion, from x1 through x2, x4, x8 and x16 to x32 in the base game. One dry spin with no colossal or Battle symbol on screen knocks it all the way back to x1.

Free Spins swap the ladder for a steeper one: x1, x4, x16, x64, x256 and x1024, still reset by a losing spin. Super Free Spins use the same ladder but it never resets, which is what makes x1024 reachable instead of decorative. A random Multiplier Upgrade can also fire after the first base-game spin and swap in the free-spins ladder early.

Colossal symbols and the Battle feature

Colossal symbols land as 2x2 blocks. When one is part of a win it explodes along with every matching symbol anywhere on the reels, and if the surface below is uneven it crushes symbols until the surface is level. On a losing spin, all colossals and their matching symbols clear the board together, so a spin that pays nothing can still sweep the layout and trigger another climb.

Wilds expand to cover their whole reel, and a colossal on that reel becomes a colossal Wild. Stack a Wild on another and it carries an x2 multiplier, with each further stack adding +1 up to x10. The Battle feature fires when a Battle symbol lands at the end of a losing spin with no colossals on screen: up to three Wilds or two colossal symbols drop in to cover it, and every one of them can extend the run before the feature ends.

Free Spins, Super Free Spins and Elevate

Landing 3, 4, 5 or 6 Bonus symbols awards 8, 10, 12 or 14 Free Spins; if at least one of them is a Super Bonus symbol, Super Free Spins start instead. Both rounds can retrigger, and colossals, Wilds and Battle symbols stay live throughout.

The Elevate menu sells shortcuts at prices that scale with the bet: Bonus Hunt (2x the bet for a 2x bonus chance), Bonus Hunt Extreme (10x for a 7x chance), Multiplier Upgrade (25x), Battle Feature (50x), Bonus (100x) and Super Bonus (300x).
