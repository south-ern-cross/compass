Super Golden Spins (Pragmatic Play): a 3x3 classic with two prize wheels and a 2,700x ceiling

Published 8 November 2026. Pragmatic Play released Super Golden Spins on 5 November 2026, and it is a deliberate step backwards in the best sense: a 3x3 stepper with nine paylines, red sevens, single, double and triple BARs, gemstones, and two golden prize wheels standing on either side of the grid. No cascades, no expanding reels, no feature ladder. The whole game is one question: how often will three golden wheel scatters land, and what will the two wheels pay when they do?

A stepper that keeps it simple

Nine fixed paylines cross the 3x3 grid, so every spin plays the full board and there is nothing to configure before the first spin. The paytable reads like a land-based stepper: single, double and triple BARs, red sevens and gemstones, plus multiplier symbols marked x2, x3, x5 and x7 that pay as line symbols. The provider page adds one detail that sets the base game apart from a plain fruit machine: certain three-symbol combinations across the nine paylines award instant wins of up to 700x. With a hit frequency of about one spin in three in the vendor math sheet, most sessions will see regular small line hits rather than long dry spells, which is exactly what low volatility means in practice.

Two wheels decide the bonus

The scatter is a golden prize wheel icon. Three of them anywhere on the grid trigger the bonus, and the grid fades away while two independent prize wheels take the screen, one on each side. Each wheel holds money values from 5x to 100x the total bet and multipliers of x2, x3, x5, x7, x10 and x27. Both wheels spin, and the combination decides the prize: two money values are added together, one multiplier boosts the money value from the other wheel, and if both wheels land on multipliers, the two values multiply each other before multiplying the total bet. The global cap is 2,700x, so even the strongest combination is cut off there. The vendor math sheet puts the bonus trigger at about one spin in 128, so the wheels stay a rare event even in a game that pays often in the base game.

No buys, no ante bet

There is no shortcut to the wheels. The coverage drawn from the vendor paytable and game files lists no bonus buy cost, no ante bet toggle and no separate RTP for a buy mode, so Super Golden Spins is a plain spin-to-trigger game: the only way into the bonus is to land three scatters. That fits the rest of the design. A low-volatility 3x3 with a 96.55% RTP is built for steady sessions and frequent small wins, not for paying to skip to the feature, and the 2,700x ceiling is modest next to the five-figure max wins on the provider's bigger releases. The trade is clear: less waiting around for a big moment, and a smaller top prize when it arrives.

What to check before playing

Two numbers deserve a second look. One listing calls the volatility high, but the launch coverage, the vendor paytable and the other listings all say low, so the table follows the majority and the outlier is worth knowing about rather than repeating. The headline RTP of 96.55% is also only one of three configurations, with 95.52% and 94.53% in circulation, so the setting at your casino is worth checking before a long session. The release date is consistent across the listings at 5 November 2026, and the 2,700x max win is agreed everywhere. Stakes run from 0.10 to 250, which leaves room for small sessions as well as larger ones.
