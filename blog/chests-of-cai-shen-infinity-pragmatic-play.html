Chests of Cai Shen – Infinity (Pragmatic Play): four coin chests feed a Hold and Win bonus with a 15,000x ceiling

Published 24 October 2026. Pragmatic Play's Chests of Cai Shen – Infinity lands on 26 October 2026, a new Chests of Cai Shen title from the studio. The 5x3, 25-line base game keeps the misty river valley with pagodas and drifting boats, but the collection engine now runs on four colored coin types, each feeding its own chest above the reels. Any chest can crack the respin round open, and the modifiers stack. 96.50% RTP, high volatility, 15,000x max win.

Four chests above the reels

The base game is a straight 5x3 with 25 fixed paylines, set on a misty Chinese river valley with pagodas on both banks and boats drifting past. Card ranks 10 through A pay the small stuff; rabbits, pigs, fish and a smiling Cai Shen, the god of wealth, pay more. The point of every spin sits above the grid: four chests marked Prosperity (green), Fulfillment (blue), Double (red) and Longevity (purple). Colored coins land on the reels and fly up into the matching chest, and any coin can randomly trigger the respin round. Even dead spins feed the chests, which is what keeps the session moving.

Hold and Win with four modifiers

The bonus is a Hold and Win respin round on its own special grid. It opens with three respins; every sticky money symbol that lands locks in place and resets the counter to three. Money symbols carry values up to 150x the bet. Which chest triggered the round decides the modifier: green Prosperity raises the values of up to three money symbols, blue Fulfillment adds four multiplier zones to the grid, purple Longevity grants an extra respin so the counter starts at four, and red Double splits the board into two separate 5x3 grids. Trigger more than one chest and the modifiers stack - the Quadruple Bonus runs all four at once, two grids, extra spins, multiplier zones and value upgrades together. Fill all 15 positions on either grid and the game awards the 5,000x Grand on top.

Check the RTP version

Two buy buttons sit beside the reels: one for the Fulfillment Bonus, one for the full Quad Bonus with all four modifiers at once. Pragmatic Play games ship in multiple RTP configurations, so the in-game information screen at a licensed casino is the figure that matters.
