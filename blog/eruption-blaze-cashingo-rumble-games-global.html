Eruption Blaze Cashingo Rumble (Games Global): the volcano slot where a second grid does the paying, with Hot Mode, a 12-prize wheel and 500x free spins

Published 31 October 2026. Games Global and Slingshot Studios released Eruption Blaze Cashingo Rumble on 29 October 2026, the third Slingshot entry in the Cashingo series and the second game in the Eruption Blaze line. The idea is simple to watch and slow to pay off: coins land on the reels, move to a second grid beside them, and stay there until a full line closes. The reels themselves are almost decoration. A spin that wins nothing on the ways can still be the spin that sets up the next payout.

The grid beside the reels

Wins on the ways pay left to right, but the paytable is modest on purpose: five 777s pay 4x the bet, five bells 3x, five cherries 2x. The real game lives in the coins. Yellow and blue coins land on the reels carrying random values from 0.2x to 10x the bet. Blue cash coins and blue jackpot coins move to the matching position on the Cashingo grid, and values stack when a coin is already there. Complete a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line and the set pays out. Up to four sets can complete on a single spin, coins at crossings count for every line they touch, and only coins that formed part of a winning set are cleared. Everything else stays, so the board keeps building between payouts.

Wilds appear on reels two to five and stand in for every symbol except scatters, coins and bonus tokens. They pay nothing on their own, which fits the design: the ways game is the waiting room, the grid is the game.

Coins, sequences and four jackpots

There is a second way for coins to pay without waiting for a line. Land two or more cash coins in sequence from the leftmost reel and their combined value pays at once, and a jackpot coin inside a winning sequence pays its jackpot instead. The four jackpots inside the grid are GRAND at 5,000x, MAJOR at 250x, MINOR at 50x and MINI at 15x. Two rules keep them rare: only one of each jackpot coin can sit on the grid at a time, and a jackpot coin can never be the coin that completes a set. The jackpots ride in on lines you were already building.

Two small modifiers keep coins coming in the base game. The volcano can erupt a coin onto the reels at random, and a collection pot grows as coins land and can scatter more across the reels. The pot itself is purely visual and changes nothing.

Hot Mode, on a timer or out of nowhere

Hot Mode is the feature that changes the shape of a session. It arrives in one of two ways: at random during the base game, awarding five to ten paid spins, or when a 24-hour countdown finishes, awarding ten. While it runs, only orbs land on the reels, every other symbol becomes a blank, and extra orbs are added, so the odds of completing a Cashingo set go up. The countdown keeps running even if you leave the game, and the buy feature is switched off while Hot Mode runs.

The booster, and free spins fixed at 500x

Scatters that fail still do something. One or two scatters in the base game feed the Cashingo Booster, a meter that collects a multiplier for the next free spins round. It starts at 10x and climbs by 10x each time the meter fills, up to a 200x ceiling. When free spins begin, that multiplier is spread across two to 20 unique grid positions, and any cash coin in a winning set on a multiplied position pays the boosted value. Each position multiplier clears once used, and jackpots can never be multiplied.

Three scatters award eight free spins. If one of the three is a Super Scatter, the round becomes 500x Free Spins instead: a flat 500x Cashingo multiplier spread across the grid before the first spin, and unlike the standard round, it does not empty the booster when it ends. Plus-one and plus-two symbols add spins during the round, and the round cannot be retriggered. After a standard round, the booster empties and resets to 10x.

The wheel, and the paid shortcuts

The Wheel Bonus is a slow collection job. Gather at least one bonus token in each of the five rows, across as many spins as it takes, and the wheel spins for one of 12 guaranteed prizes: the four jackpots, free spins or 500x free spins, with multipliers on some prizes. After the wheel stops, the volcano can nudge the result up or down by up to two segments. The top wheel prize is 5,000x, the GRAND jackpot, which cannot be multiplied.

Paid options sit around the free spins round. The buy feature jumps straight to free spins, priced by the starting Cashingo multiplier: 50x for a 50x multiplier, 90x for 100x, 175x for 200x and 450x for 500x. The Upsizer, offered after a round triggers, sells a higher starting multiplier before the first spin, and Bonus Recharge sells one extra feature spin at the end of a round. None of these are offered everywhere, and some are switched off above certain bets, so the menu depends on the casino and the region.

What to check before playing

Two numbers deserve a second look. The provider press release gives the 96% RTP for the Eruption Blaze series and the jackpot and wheel prizes, but no overall max win, so the 9,000x ceiling in the table comes from the game listing, not the provider. And the 96% figure is the series figure, so check the game panel at your casino for the active setting. The other thing to know before spinning is the pace: this is a game about watching a board fill, with long quiet stretches where nothing lands. If you want your wins from the reels themselves, the ways game here will feel thin. If you like a slow build toward a line closing four ways at once, that is exactly what this one sells.
