What is new in Money Train 5

The headline change is the two-mode structure. Standard Mode caps the max win at 50,000x, while Ultra Mode doubles it to 100,000x - Relax split the math model instead of simply chasing a bigger number than Money Train 4's 150,000x.

In the base game, the new Nuclear Wild can trigger on any spin: crosshairs lock onto up to five reels and detonate them into full wild reels.

The bonus round keeps the Money Cart respin format but adds station progression. Ticket symbols push the train through stations: Station 5 unlocks persistent symbols, Station 10 adds an extra row, Station 15 brings the new Bomber and Pyromancer characters, and Station 20 activates the Permanent Payer. New faces include the Ticket Master, which can start the bonus directly at Station 15.
