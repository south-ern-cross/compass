What is new in Le Vampire

The Le series formula returns with a vampiric twist. Winning positions are highlighted as Golden Squares; Rainbow or Epic Rainbow symbols then activate them to reveal Coins worth up to 500x the bet, multiplying Clover symbols, and collecting Bag of Gold and Pot of Gold symbols. New Blood Squares reveal higher-value Coins than the golden ones.

Three free spins modes - Coffin Break, Love At First Bite and Do Your Fang - add symbol upgrades and persistent highlighted squares, with guaranteed Rainbows on every free spin in the top mode. The math pairs medium volatility (3 out of 5) with a 15,000x ceiling, and the headline 97.57% RTP is unusually high for a new release - though most casinos will run one of the lower configurations.
