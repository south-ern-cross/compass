What is new in Demonic Dolls

The whole game revolves around the Wild Rattle symbol. Wild Rattles carry additive multipliers up to 100x that apply to any win they join, and triggering the respins feature relocates them to new grid positions. Two Wild Rattles that were part of a win merge into a 2x2 Colossal Wild Rattle with a combined multiplier; four merge into two separate Colossal Wild Rattles.

Three free spins modes raise the Wild Rattle frequency, with one or two guaranteed on each free spin. Hit frequency is quoted at 39.8%, so roughly two spins in five produce a win. The math is medium volatility with a default RTP of 96.26% and a maximum payout of 10,000x the bet, reachable in all game modes.
