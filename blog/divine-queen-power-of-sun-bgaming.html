Divine Queen: Power of Sun (BGaming): x128 Cell Multipliers, 97% RTP and 5,000x Max Win

Published 6 October 2026. BGaming's Divine Queen: Power of Sun is live: a follow-up to last November's streamer favorite Divine Queen: Heart of Ice that trades ice for a sun-scorched Egypt, where empty cells left by winning symbols grow multipliers that double all the way to x128.

How it plays

Wins land when eight or more matching symbols appear anywhere on the grid, and winning symbols clear through cascades. When they disappear, a multiplier cell appears in the empty spot - and if that cell takes part in another win, the multiplier doubles, climbing from x2 all the way to x128.

The Sun Goddess doubles as the scatter: four or more trigger free spins. A Super Free Spins variant starts with an x2 multiplier already sitting in every cell, so the first cascade can pay from a loaded grid. Players who do not want to wait can buy their way in - a Chance x10 option raises the odds of triggering the bonus naturally, Boosters seed multiplier cells onto the grid, and both the free spins and the super free spins are available as direct purchases where supported.
