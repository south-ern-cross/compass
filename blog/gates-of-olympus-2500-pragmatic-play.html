What is new in Gates of Olympus 2500

The headline change is the wheel. Landing 4 to 6 scatters awards 15 free spins, but before the feature begins a wheel spins to decide which of four bonus games you get. No previous Gates of Olympus did this: earlier sequels just raised a number and called it a day.

The four games play differently. In Free Spins 1, multipliers landing on winning spins are added to a progressive total. In Free Spins 2, every multiplier symbol on screen gets collected. Super Free Spins 1 and 2 lift the minimum multiplier to 10x. Landing 3 scatters during the feature adds 5 more free spins.

The base game keeps the series formula: 6x5 grid, 8 or more matching symbols anywhere pay, tumbling wins, and multiplier orbs up to 2,500x that combine at the end of the spin. New betting tools join in: Super Spin 1 guarantees a multiplier on every spin, Super Spin 2 raises the minimum to 25x, plus three Ante Bet options and four bonus buy options that jump straight into any of the bonus modes.
