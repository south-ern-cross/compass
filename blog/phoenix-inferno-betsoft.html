Phoenix Inferno (Betsoft): the legendary bird rises over 720 ways

Published 17 October 2026. Betsoft put Phoenix Inferno out on 15 October, a fire-and-fantasy slot built around the legendary bird. The unusual 3-4-5-4-3 reel layout offers 720 ways to win, with CASH symbols as the headline mechanic. RTP: 96.07%, medium volatility.

Fire meets fortune

The phoenix is one of the most reused images in slots, and Betsoft leans into it: the legendary bird rises above the reels in a game built around power and reward. The setting mixes fire and fantasy, with CASH symbols spread across the grid as the main route to the bigger payouts.

A 3-4-5-4-3 grid with 720 ways

Instead of the standard five-by-three, Phoenix Inferno runs on a 3-4-5-4-3 layout: three symbols on the outer reels, five in the middle. That shape produces 720 ways to win on every spin, paying on adjacent reels from the left.

CASH symbols bring the heat

CASH symbols are the headline feature, carrying instant prizes that land on top of the ways wins. The official screenshots also show Mini, Minor, Major and Mega jackpot tiers, a multiplier meter, a spins counter and a Buy Bonus button. Backing it all is the usual Betsoft toolkit: wilds, scatters, free spins and multipliers, in a mobile-first HTML5 build.

Betsoft's October run

Phoenix Inferno follows Birds in The City, 3 Pots of Venice and THE Queen of Social Media in Betsoft’s recent release run, and sits ahead of November’s THE Collector and THE Aristocrats. The studio keeps a steady cadence, and the phoenix is its October headline.
