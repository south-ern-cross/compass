The AML policy's geo-blocking section says users are checked so they are not subject to sanctions from Austria, France and its territories, Germany, the Netherlands and its territories, Spain, the Union of Comoros, the United Kingdom, the USA and its territories, all FATF-blacklisted countries, and any other jurisdiction the Anjouan Offshore Financial Authority deems prohibited. On BC.Game's provider page, NetEnt says it will not supply NetEnt games to an operator that operates in listed jurisdictions unless that operator holds the licences NetEnt requires. The 2024 Remote Gambling Bill's explanatory text says that amendment has not been brought into operation and that interactive gambling is not currently regulated in South Africa. Licensing & Safety

BC.Game's AML page says the site is operated by Twocent Technology Limited, a company registered in Belize under number 000041939, with its registered address at Sea Urchin Street, San Pedro, Ambergris Caye, Belize. The same page says the site is licensed by the Government of the Autonomous Island of Anjouan, Union of Comoros, under licence No. ALSI-202410011-FI1.

The General Terms print that company number as 41939 and add "Sea Urchin Street #2" to the address. They name the same Anjouan licence number. They also say the agreement is governed by the laws of Curacao, and that the courts of Curacao hear disputes. That governing-law clause is not the same thing as a Curaçao gambling licence.

On 29 September 2026, the Anjouan seal check for the domain bc.game showed Twocent Technology Limited and licence No. ALSI-202410011-FI1, issued by the Offshore Finance Authority of the State of Anjouan under the Computer Gaming Licensing Act 007 of 2005, with supervision by Anjouan Licensing Services Inc. The status line on that page said VALID. The same page warned that a seal generated on 12 January 2026 had expired and told the reader to refresh the seal. Read that result as a check on that date, not as a certificate that stays valid on its own.

That validation page says the Anjouan licence does not extend to Australia, Comoros, France, the Netherlands, Spain, the United Kingdom, the USA, FATF-blacklisted countries, sanctioned jurisdictions, or any other territory the Anjouan Offshore Financial Authority excludes. It says complaints from players in those places fall outside the licence.

The General Terms allow BC.Game to ask for KYC documents at any time, and to hold service and payment until identity is verified. Players must be 18, or older where local law sets a higher age. The User Agreement says the operator's maximum liability arising from use of the site will not exceed €100, where the law allows that cap. Help text on the terms page says self-exclusion locks the account for at least 6 months, and that a 7-day cooling-off period is also available.

Payment Methods

The User Agreement says deposits and withdrawals must use cryptocurrency that is valid and belongs to the player. It also says cryptocurrency is not legal tender on the site and that its value can move sharply. The General Terms say you may not use a payment method that belongs to another person.

The public help pages describe deposits as instant and withdrawals as fast. That is the operator's wording. This review did not time a payout and does not list coins.

Bonus Rules

No welcome percentage or bonus cap is stated here. The public offer pages did not show one set of figures together with the rules, so no bonus amount is published on this page.

The User Agreement, section 5, does say this. If you take a deposit bonus, the original deposit cannot be withdrawn until that offer's wagering requirement is met. If you withdraw before a promotion's rules are met, BC.Game says it will deduct the full bonus and any winnings tied to it. A bonus can be received once per person, account, household, address, email address or shared IP. BC.Game can cancel, refuse or reclaim a promotion, and it can ask for identity documents before it credits promotional money. Where a promotion has its own terms, those terms prevail if they conflict with the User Agreement.
