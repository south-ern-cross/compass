How It Works

Expected value = bonus amount − (wagering required × house edge). The house edge is simply 100% − RTP, and wagering is adjusted for game contribution: a game that counts 10% forces you to wager ten times more to clear the same bonus. High wagering on low-RTP games turns most "generous" bonuses negative.
