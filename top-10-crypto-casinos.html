What is a crypto casino?

A crypto casino is an online casino that accepts cryptocurrency deposits and pays withdrawals back in crypto. Most accept Bitcoin, Ethereum and stablecoins like USDT. Crypto payments skip the banking system, so deposits credit fast and withdrawals typically arrive much sooner than fiat payouts.

The casinos we rank hold verifiable licenses and have a public track record. That said, offshore crypto casinos do not carry the protection of a strict national regulator. Cloudbet's welcome package of up to $2,500 is paid as cash rewards - rakeback, daily and weekly cash prizes - with no wagering requirements attached. The rewards are real cash you can withdraw, not bonus funds you must play through.

What is a wagering requirement?

How many times you must bet a bonus amount before withdrawing it. A 35x requirement on 100 USDT means 3,500 USDT in bets. Use our Wagering Calculator to see what any bonus really costs.

Do crypto casinos require ID verification?

Policies differ. Some crypto casinos let you play with just an email, others ask for ID before the first withdrawal or once you pass a threshold. The honest answer: check the casino's Terms and AML policy before depositing, and assume a large win can trigger a verification check anywhere.

Can I play with USDT?
