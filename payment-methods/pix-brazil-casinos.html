How PIX Works

PIX is Brazil's instant payment system, launched by the Central Bank in 2020. It lets you send money between bank accounts in seconds, 24/7, using a PIX key (your CPF, phone number, email, or a random key) or a QR code. For casino players, it replaced slow bank slips (boletos) and expensive card deposits as the default way to fund an account.

Since April 2026, SPA-licensed casinos in Brazil are largely required to offer PIX as a deposit method. Offshore casinos serving Brazilian players also support it, though availability varies.

Fees: Who Charges What

The Central Bank does not charge players for PIX transfers. Your bank should not charge you either. If the casino charges a deposit fee, that is unusual and worth questioning. Some casinos limit free withdrawals (for example, 3 free per month) and charge a small flat fee after that. Check the payments or terms page before you play.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PIX free to use at online casinos?

In most cases, yes. The Central Bank does not charge players for PIX transfers, and most casinos absorb any processing cost. If a casino tries to charge you a fee for a PIX deposit, treat that as a red flag and check our methodology page for how we score payment fairness.

How fast are PIX casino deposits?

Deposits are typically instant, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. If your deposit has not arrived within 15 minutes, contact the casino's live chat with your transaction receipt.

Can I withdraw my winnings with PIX?

Yes. PIX works both ways. Withdrawals via PIX are typically processed within a few hours, though the casino may have its own internal review period of up to 24 hours before releasing the transfer.

Do I need a Brazilian bank account to use PIX?

Yes. PIX is tied to accounts at Brazilian banks and fintechs. If you do not have one, crypto (USDT or BTC) is the usual alternative at offshore casinos that accept Brazilian players.

Is PIX safe for casino payments?

PIX itself is run by the Central Bank of Brazil and uses bank-grade authentication. The risk is not the payment rail but the casino: only deposit at licensed operators. See our guide to online casinos in Brazil for how to verify a license.

What is the minimum PIX deposit at casinos?

Typically R$20-50, though it varies by operator. This is a typical range, not a guarantee - always check the cashier page before depositing.
