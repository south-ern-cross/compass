Bitcoin Casino Guide: Deposits, Fees and Speed

How to fund a casino account with Bitcoin (BTC): the deposit steps, real fees, confirmation times and what BTC does and does not hide about you.

How to deposit Bitcoin at a casino

  1. Get a Bitcoin wallet - any reputable mobile or desktop wallet works.
  2. Buy BTC on an exchange or peer-to-peer, then move it to your own wallet.
  3. Open the casino cashier, choose Bitcoin and copy the deposit address.
  4. Send from your wallet to that address. Double-check it - transactions cannot be reversed.
  5. Wait for confirmations. Most casinos credit the balance after 1-2 confirmations.

Fees and speed

The network fee goes to Bitcoin miners, not to the casino. Fees rise when the network is busy and fall when it is quiet - your wallet shows the current rate before you send. Typical deposit time is 10-60 minutes. Withdrawals are usually 10 minutes to a few hours after the casino approves the request, far faster than a bank wire.

Privacy: what BTC actually hides

Bitcoin is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Every transaction is permanently public on the blockchain, and the casino links deposits to your account. Assume any casino can ask for ID verification (KYC) before a large withdrawal - "crypto casino" does not mean "no questions ever".

FAQ

How long does a Bitcoin casino deposit take?

Usually 10-60 minutes. Most casinos credit your balance after 1-2 blockchain confirmations. When the Bitcoin network is busy, it can take longer - the casino cannot speed this up.

Are Bitcoin casino deposits anonymous?

No - pseudonymous. Every transaction is public on the blockchain. The casino still knows your account, and most casinos can ask for ID verification before a withdrawal.

What happens if I send BTC to the wrong address?

Bitcoin transactions are irreversible. Always copy the address from the casino cashier (never type it), send a small test amount first, and double-check the first and last characters.

Do casinos charge fees on Bitcoin deposits?

Reputable casinos do not add their own fee, but you always pay the Bitcoin network (miner) fee from your wallet. Check the fee your wallet suggests before sending.

Can I withdraw my winnings in Bitcoin too?

Yes, at crypto casinos withdrawals go back in crypto. They are typically much faster than bank payouts - often minutes to a few hours after the casino approves the request.
