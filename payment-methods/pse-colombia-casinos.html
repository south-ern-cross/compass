How PSE Works

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Linea) is Colombia's online banking payment gateway, connecting all major Colombian banks through a single interface. When you pay with PSE, you are redirected to your own bank's secure portal to authorize the transfer. The merchant (casino) never sees your banking password.

PSE is the default online payment method in Colombia for everything from utilities to e-commerce, and it is the standard deposit method at Coljuegos-licensed casinos.

Fees: Who Charges What

PSE itself does not charge the player. Your bank treats it as a regular transfer. Casinos should not add fees on PSE deposits. As with all methods, check the casino's payments page for any withdrawal fee policy (for example, a limited number of free withdrawals per month).

What is PSE and how do I use it at online casinos?

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Linea) is Colombia's secure online banking gateway. At the casino cashier you select PSE, pick your bank from the list, log in through your bank's portal, and approve the transfer. You return to the casino and the funds appear almost immediately.

Is PSE required at Colombian casinos?

Coljuegos-licensed operators must offer payment methods accessible to Colombian players, and PSE is the standard. Offshore casinos serving Colombia also commonly support it.

How fast are PSE casino deposits?

Near-instant. The transfer itself takes seconds once you approve it in your bank portal. If funds do not appear within 15 minutes, contact support.

Can I withdraw winnings with PSE?

Yes. PSE withdrawals go back to your Colombian bank account. Allow 1-24 hours for casino processing, then the transfer itself is fast.

What is the minimum PSE deposit?

What if I do not have a Colombian bank account?
