PSE in Colombia: deposits and withdrawal checks

TL;DR: PSE (Pagos Seguros en Línea) is an online payment service from ACH Colombia that debits an eligible bank account or electronic deposit. Casino availability must be checked separately; a PSE deposit does not confirm a PSE withdrawal.

How to check and make a PSE deposit

  1. Check the exact operator domain in the Coljuegos register and confirm that Colombia is allowed in its terms.
  2. Confirm that PSE appears in the cashier for your account, then check the currency, limits and any charges.
  3. Select your financial institution and authorize the debit through its official banking flow. Never share banking passwords with casino support.
  4. Save the transaction reference and check its status before trying again. Contact your bank and the operator if the payment remains pending.

Do not assume PSE withdrawals are available

PSE describes its service as online purchases and payments debited from an account. This is not confirmation that a casino can return winnings through PSE. Ask the operator to confirm the actual withdrawal method, identity checks, approval time, fees and limits before depositing. We do not list specific casinos as verified PSE providers on this page.

Frequently asked questions

Does a PSE logo prove that a casino is licensed in Colombia?

No. Verify the exact domain in the Coljuegos register. A payment method or foreign licence does not replace local authorization.

Can I withdraw winnings with PSE?

Do not assume so. Confirm the separate withdrawal method offered for your account and its terms before making a deposit.

What is the minimum PSE deposit?

There is no universal casino deposit minimum stated here. Check the amount shown by the operator and the transaction limits set by your financial institution.
